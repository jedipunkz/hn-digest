Save useful AI answers with one click, then organize them with tags and full-text search in your browser.
SaveAnswer adds a Save button to supported AI chat pages, so you can keep useful answers, tag them, and find them later.
Your saved answers stay in local browser storage. No account, no cloud sync, no tracking.
What you can do with SaveAnswer:
- Save useful AI answers with one click
- Add tags when saving an answer
- Search saved questions and answers by keyword
- Filter saved answers by tag
- Keep rich formatting such as code blocks, lists and tables
- Reopen the original conversation source link
- Build a small personal AI knowledge library across multiple platforms
Who it is for:
- Developers saving code snippets, debugging notes and architecture ideas
- Students saving explanations, summaries and study material
- Researchers collecting AI-generated notes and references
- Writers saving outlines, drafts and creative ideas
- Anyone who uses AI chat tools every day and wants to reuse the best answers later
Privacy:
SaveAnswer is local-first. Your saved content is stored in your browser through chrome.storage.local. The extension does not require an account, does not upload your saved answers to a server and does not track your usage.
Open source:
SaveAnswer is open source, so users can inspect how it works and report issues or feature requests.
