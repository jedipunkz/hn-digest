AI + Human Collaboration Via Human Tool Mediated Loop (HTML??)
In this post we introduce one approach to facilitating tight human + bot collaboration that we think offers exciting possibilities.
As opposed to human-in-the-loop, where in a human is looped into an AI driven flow, we show an approach that has both the bot and the human in a tight loop having a back-and-forth interaction mediated via a human tool. Human tool in this case simply means a UI application, within Solvent, it means web apps.
Conceptually, one can think of this approach as bringing the CoPilot mode of AI integration into more general human workflows. CoPilot generally means integrating AI into existing software products and systems (excel, docs, Adobe...etc).
With HTML (my hands are up, don't shoot 😉), the CoPilot approach can apply to general human workflows that are not tied to any specific product.
Of course you can hook AI into any piece of software you create now but we think an integrated platform approach for something such as this works much better, ie the platform provides all the bells are whistles involved into integrating AI and developer (maybe Citizen Developer/VibeCoder) can focus only on creating the human tool functionality.
