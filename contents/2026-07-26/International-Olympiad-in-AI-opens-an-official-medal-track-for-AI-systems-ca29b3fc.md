FAQ: AI モデル トラックにおける人間の役割は何ですか?

システムが実行を開始した後は、人間がモデルの答えを解決、修正、デバッグ、編集、または何らかの形で改善することはできません。モデル自体は、IOAI から提供された認証情報を使用して (MCP 経由で) 送信を行う必要があります。モデルの現在の出力を監視し、依存する API が失敗した場合はバックエンド サービスを再起動できます。これらの制限は、

FAQ: What is the role of humans in the AI Models Track?
The AI Models track is intended to be solved by AI systems autonomously, not interactively with human input. A human operator may only initiate execution of the AI system with a short starting prompt (provided by IOAI). If at any point the system stops on its own, a human operator may only enter another “continuation” prompt (provided by IOAI) to bring the system out of a stopped state and continue its execution.
A human may not solve, correct, debug, edit, or in any way improve the model’s answers after the system has begun its execution. The model itself must make the submission (via MCP), using credentials provided by IOAI. You may monitor the model’s current output and restart backend services if a dependent API fails. These restrictions ensure we measure long-running AI model behavior.

FAQ: What is an “AI system”? Which AI systems can take part?
An “AI System” is any combination of an AI model (LLM, diffusion model etc), an agentic harness to support the AI model to write and execute code via tools, and an agentic orchestration approach the agent executes.
Each AI system must run on the registering organization’s hardware and submit solution code via Kaggle. IOAI does not prohibit the hardware used for executing this AI system, nor bear its costs; any system may be used. The solution code submitted to our platform will run on standardized Kaggle hardware with one GPU.
FAQ: How are AI systems scores verified by IOAI?
Within 24 hours of the Day One and Day Two session, registering AI systems will be able to see their private percentile scores on each task. At this point, they have 48 hours to make a decision on whether they wish to verify their scores, or appear on the leaderboard pseudonymously. Only verified scores with an organization’s name are eligible for official IOAI Gold, Silver and Bronze medals.
If opting to verify scores, then over the subsequent few weeks, the registering organization must submit confidential execution traces of both sessions. These traces should include timestamped AI model inputs and outputs, tool-call inputs and outputs, tools-call definitions, subagent execution traces, code execution outputs, libraries used, and any human-written system prompts. We request they be anonymized to redact information like local hardware IP addresses.
As we recognize these execution traces may contain proprietary information or secret sauce, they will be treated as confidential and not be shared beyond the IOAI Jury members assigned for verification. Within 4 weeks of submitting the execution traces, the IOAI Jury will announce Gold, Silver and Bronze medalists.
FAQ: Is there a restriction on which AI models may be used? My organization has unreleased models we wish to benchmark.
All AI models are welcome: open-weight models, proprietary models with public access through APIs, and proprietary models with restricted or no public access. Each organization may enter up to three AI systems, of which at most one may use restricted AI models. During registration, each AI system should be given a distinct identifier for tracking purposes.
FAQ: Who owns the intellectual property rights to submissions?
Registering organizations retain full ownership of their AI models, harness software, system prompts, documentation, execution traces and other artifacts produced on the registering organization’s hardware during the IOAI AI Models Track.
By taking part in the AI Models Track, you agree that the percentile score will be included in the leaderboard (under the organization name or under a pseudonym, as specified above) and subsequent IOAI publications for non-commercial scientific research.
