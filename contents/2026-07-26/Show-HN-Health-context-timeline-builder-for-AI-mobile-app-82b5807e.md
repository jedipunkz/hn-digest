Download Kurozu: AI Health Context by YIFAN WU on the App Store. See screenshots, ratings and reviews, user tips, and more apps like Kurozu: AI Health Context.

Working on a mobile app for making reusable AI-ready health context - Simply a timeline of events/facts
- Events are natural language, with fuzzy dates
- Simple Apple Health insight extraction
- Export as prompt
- Local first, no account required The thinking is that, for most health queries to AI I want my personal health context to be part of the input. And don't want to rewrite every time. Curious whether this approach makes sense to other people.
Try the app and share your thoughts!

Kurozu helps you build a local-first timeline of health context specifically for enhancing communication with AI.
Start locally without an account. Add facts with precise, approximate, or timeless dates. When you choose to sign in, cloud timelines can sync through your account.
