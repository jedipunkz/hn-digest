Observability has traditionally been tacked on after your code hits production, but with agentic operations on the rise, that's no longer sustainable.
Agents have dramatically increased the rate of change as they write more code, ship more changes, and operate more systems—all at a speed that compounds scale and complexity. As a result, observability needs to be factored in before deployment, with instrumentation written alongside the code, agents evaluated before they ship, and plans informed by production data.
AI Week is designed to help you navigate that shift. We’re dropping big announcements every day about new AI capabilities in Grafana Assistant , the agentic LLM in Grafana Cloud, and our growing suite of AI tools that will help you test your code before you ship as well as investigate incidents, evaluate agents, and automate routine operational work.

Agent Observability: Generally available on Thursday, Agent Observability extends Grafana Cloud's OpenTelemetry-native monitoring to the AI systems teams are now shipping. It watches your agents, tests their behavior, and steps in when something's off. Instrumented agents emit telemetry that captures the usual signals (usage, latency, and errors) alongside newer signals that matter specifically for AI systems: token usage/cost and the conversation itself. Conversation data gives teams forensic-level debugging down to the individual exchange. Built-in evaluators go a step further, continuously scoring conversations to check agent behavior directly while guards enforce your policies in real time, blocking unwanted behavior, such as hallucinations, drift, or policy violation, before a customer does.
