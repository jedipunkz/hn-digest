QuickOn is an AI lead-qualification platform — the intelligence layer between any lead source and any CRM. It receives inbound leads, enriches them, qualifies them with an explainable AI score and grade, recommends the next sales action, and can sync the result to your CRM.
Each lead is scored against a 10-criterion rubric aligned to your Ideal Customer Profile: ICP match, decision-maker authority, buying intent, company size, industry fit, urgency, geography, contact completeness, business maturity, and website quality. Objective criteria are scored deterministically in code; subjective ones by your chosen AI model. The result is a 0–100 score, an A–D grade, a confidence level, and written reasoning.
Sources include website forms, the REST API, webhooks, Typeform, Jotform, Google Forms, Zapier, Make, CSV upload, and a drop-in JavaScript widget. CRM sync ships with HubSpot, and Pipedrive, Salesforce, Zoho, and Freshsales are adapter-only additions.
