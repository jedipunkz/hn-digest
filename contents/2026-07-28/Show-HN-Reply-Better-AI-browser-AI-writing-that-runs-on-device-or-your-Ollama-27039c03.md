• オンデバイス、無料、プライベート。組み込みの Gemini Nano モデルを使用して Chrome でローカルに実行します。キーもアカウントも必要なく、テキストがコンピュータから離れることはありません。サポートされている Chrome デスクトップが必要で、モデルは最初の使用時に 1 回ダウンロードされます。

• Groq、無料かつ高速。独自の無料の Groq キーを追加して、クラウドを迅速に生成します。
• OpenRouter、500 以上のモデル。 Claude、GPT、Gemini、DeepSeek、Llama などを無料または有料で使用し、検索可能なピッカー、トークンごとのライブ価格設定、コンテキスト ウィンドウ バッジを備えています。

Improve and reply to messages with AI. Free and private, no key: on-device or a local model (Ollama/LM Studio). Or your own key.
Reply Better AI helps you write better anywhere on the web. It rewrites your drafts and writes replies in context, using the AI engine of your choice, and it is free to use with no setup.
ENGINES (pick one, or leave it on Auto)
• On-device, free and private. Runs locally in Chrome using the built-in Gemini Nano model. No key, no account, and your text never leaves your computer. Needs a supported Chrome desktop, and the model downloads once on first use.
• Local (Ollama / LM Studio), free and private. Run your own model on your machine with Ollama, LM Studio, or any OpenAI-compatible server. No key, works offline, and nothing leaves your computer. Set a preset or your own server URL in settings.
• Groq, free and fast. Add your own free Groq key for quick cloud generation.
• OpenRouter, 500+ models. Use Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Llama and more, free or paid, with a searchable picker, live per-token pricing, and context-window badges.
Auto uses on-device when your device supports it, then your Groq key, then OpenRouter, and it falls back automatically if one is busy. (Local is opt-in: pick it in settings when you want to run your own server.) The active engine is always shown, inline and in settings, so you know exactly what is running.
KEY FEATURES
• One button that morphs to fit what you are doing. Select the messages you are replying to and it becomes a Reply button; start a draft and it becomes an Improve button. It only shows on real message composers, so it stays out of search bars and form fields.
• Reply in context. Pick a tone (Match thread, Professional, Friendly, Concise, or Summarize) or type your own instruction in any language, and the reply comes back in that language.
• Live streaming results. Watch the result type itself out as it is generated, then flip to the Changes view to see exactly what was edited, word by word. Want another take? Hit Regenerate and cycle through versions, then Insert with one-tap Undo.
• Auto, Fastest free. One pick that
