Your Twitter & X bookmarks, waiting on every new tab — finally read what you saved. Search, highlight, export. Local-first.
Totem is a local-first bookmark manager for Twitter and X. It does the part other tools skip: it helps you actually read what you saved. Your new tab becomes a calm reading queue. Your saved tweets, threads, and X articles come back to you instead of getting lost.
Most people bookmark a tweet and never look at it again. Open X to find it and you lose twenty minutes to the feed. Totem fixes both. Your saved posts wait for you on every new tab. No feed. No algorithm. No noise.
EVERY NEW TAB IS YOUR READING QUEUE
• Your bookmarks load automatically every time you open a tab
• Today's Read sizes your queue to the time you have: 5, 15, or 30 minutes a day. You make progress instead of drowning.
• Every post has a clear state: Unread, Reading, or Read. Your scroll position is saved, so you pick up where you left off.
• Snooze a post for later. File one as a reference. Flag one as "action needed." Pull in two more when you're on a roll.
• Pin up to 6 favorites. Sort by recent, oldest, or annotated.
• Works from the keyboard: j/k to move, / to search, Space to open
A CLEAN READER FOR TWEETS & THREADS
• Distraction-free reader for single tweets and full threads. Threads are rebuilt in order, so you read the whole thing in one place.
• Reads X long-form Articles in full: headings, lists, images, and video
• Shows link previews, quoted tweets, reposts, images, and video
• Jump to the next or previous bookmark. Or hit "Surprise me" for a random unread.
• Add an "Open in Totem" button on X to send any tweet straight to the reader
