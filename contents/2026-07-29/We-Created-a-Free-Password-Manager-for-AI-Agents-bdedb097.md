Costs: Agents with identities increase password management tool costs.
Your password manager subscription costs you $25 and includes up to 10 members. Five human employees may have five of their own agentic employees, one each. However, what happens when thse five agentic employees each run 20 specialized jobs across research, sales, support, finance, deployments, and customer workflows? Remember, each for these 20 specialized agentic jobs should have their own service account and keypair so their access can be limited, monitored, and shut down without disrupting any other agents at work. Now, your SaaS password service bill just went up. Seren passwords is free for both humans and agents. There's zero cost to use and zero cost to scale. Every agent can have its own identity, and agent workflows can use as many identities and vaults as the work requires. Seren Passwords charges nothing per company, seat, agent, identity, or vault, so scaling agents does not turn identity and workflow growth into another software bill. Agents have independent keypairs. Each agent gets only the access it needs to do its job. When that job ends, you can stop the agent immediately without interrupting the rest of the team. Or you can immeidatel stop every agent at once, in an emergency. Agents running in containers are like employees: Their jobs/roles have a beginning and an ending. Thus, like regular employees, their access should follow the same lifecycle. Access can stay in place for a long-running agent, expire after a short task, or be cut off immediately when the job ends. As such, we should optimize to ensure that agents don't increase their budget line items as they increasingly manage multiple identities and millions of secrets. Seren Passwords is free for humans and for agents. There are no seats, pricing tiers, metering checks, or per-identity and per-vault charges. Create as many agent identities and vaults as you and your agentic workflows require, with zero incremental software cost as you scale.
Speed with Audits: Agents need fast authentication velocity
Your password manager requires multiple vaults key types and accessibility. It needs, or your administrator, to provision permissions and access. In a world in which tokens per sec (toks/sec) now matters, you LLM ain't got time for that! Password managers are optimized to slow down humans that need to read UI's and understand how they are giving themselves or other team members authentication permissions. This authentication human interaction in the UI slows down agents. Agents need pure CLI and MCP capabilities that are locked into their identities so they are not constrained by the need for human permission gating. In the pursuit of agentic speed, Agents need programmatic CLI and MCP workflows that preserve governance: Explicit, scoped, revocable grants and approval controls where the vault policy requires them. Of course, we didn't optimize for speed without awareness. Seren Passwords logs changes and secret reads that pass through the service. Human owners can see which agent acted, what it touched, and when. Secret values never appear in the log. Once a secret reaches an agent, Seren cannot see what the agent does with that secret value.
Incentives: It shouldn't matter which compute vendor provides incentives
Today, we're working with clients that are deploying agent cointainers across multiple cloud providers providing multiple incentives to attract agentic compute. Our clients want to take advantage of these incentives and that their agents must be capable of moving across containers and infrastructure without blockers and loss of secret access. Why? Well, it's all that free and juicy infra incentives that they are asking their agents to use. Compute providers all want your agent compute and container hosting business. Thus, our agents need to be able to move where the incentives are and bring their identity and their access to secrets, with them. How do they do that with Seren Passwords? Seren CLI deployed into any container and any compute allows Seren-password agents to access their secrets from anywhere and in any compute infrastructure. Your data stays yours. Seren Passwords is end-to-end encrypted, so secrets are decrypted only where your agents use them. Secrets are not decrypted by the password service and your encrypted vault data remains in your organization’s own SerenDB database. Freedom for agents to switch means our customers take full advantage of infrastructure incentives.
