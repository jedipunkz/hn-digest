Capture and annotate screenshots, organize local history, and export as PNG, JPG, PDF, or ZIP.
Screenshot Studio is a fast, local-first Chrome extension for capturing, annotating, organizing, and exporting screenshots.
Capture the visible tab or select a specific area. Screenshot Studio remembers your previous selection for quick retakes, then opens the built-in editor where you can refine the result.
Add pen marks, rectangles, circles, arrows, text, highlights, blur, pixelation, and redaction. Reposition or resize annotations, crop the image, undo changes, zoom, and pan around larger screenshots.
Every screenshot is automatically saved to your local capture history. Search by page title or domain, organize captures into folders, select multiple screenshots for bulk actions, and restore deleted items from the Recycle Bin for up to seven days.
Features:
- Capture the visible tab or a selected area
- Reuse the previous selected area
- Annotate with pen, shapes, arrows, text, and highlights
- Blur, pixelate, or redact sensitive information
- Move, resize, restyle, undo, and redo annotations
- Crop, zoom, and pan around screenshots
- Copy images directly to the clipboard
- Export individual captures as PNG, JPG, or PDF
- Combine selected captures into a multi-page PDF
- Export selected captures as a ZIP of PNG images
- Search local capture history by title or domain
- Organize captures into folders
- Restore deleted captures from the seven-day Recycle Bin
- Keep captures locally in your Chrome profile
Screenshot Studio is useful for bug reports, QA feedback, design reviews, documentation, research notes, and quick visual communication.
