AI assistants are at their best when they can read your project documents — and at their most dangerous when those documents carry things they were never meant to see. Sanitizer prepares document packages that are safe, light, and built for how AI actually reads.
Local by design. Licensed for how you use it.
Prepare company documents for AI without exposing client identities, credentials, or other sensitive data. Turn large project files into a clean, searchable knowledge base so teams get faster, more precise answers with less noise.

Use AI with your personal documents while keeping private details under your control. Sanitizer cleans, converts, and organizes files locally so your AI assistant can find the right information without reading everything.
Version 1.0.1 · Windows 10/11 (64-bit) · ~450 MB · installs per-user, no admin required.
Nothing sensitive ever reaches an AI
Every document is scrubbed on this computer, before any AI is involved: names, contacts, client identities, credentials, keys — removed or replaced with opaque tokens per your project policy. Originals and the un-redact vault stay in Sanitizer's private storage; the AI-ready workspace contains sanitized material only. In reversible mode, answers can be un-redacted locally — so the AI works blind, but you don't.
Heavy formats in, lightweight files out
A 40 MB PDF or Word file is mostly baggage: layout data, embedded fonts, metadata, binary bloat. Sanitizer converts everything to clean Markdown and flat CSV — the same content at a fraction of the size. Your AI spends its tokens and its context window on the actual engineering text, not on format garbage, which makes answers cheaper and sharper.
A searchable database, not a pile of files
Every document is chunked and indexed twice — a full-text index for exact terms, tags and standards references, and a semantic index that finds passages by meaning. AI agents get purpose-built retrieval tools automatically, so they pull the five relevant paragraphs instead of swallowing five hundred pages, and cite the page they came from.
Everything — conversion, OCR, sanitation, indexing, search — runs locally. No cloud services, no uploads, no accounts. The only thing an AI ever sees is what's left after Sanitizer is done.
