Contestants in the poster competition create unique designs that embod y many of the great things that fairgoers love about the Ohio State Fair . This year in particular, we asked artists to incorporate a patriotic theme in their designs to celebrate America's 250th anniversary .

Guidelines and Selection Criteria:
Both professional and amateur artists are permitted to enter.
Artwork must be family friendly.
In effort to help celebrate America's 250th anniversary, it is recommended that artwork include a patriotic theme, whether that is using colors of the American flag or celebrating Ohio's heritage and America 250 in some way.
Artwork must be related to the Ohio State Fair.
The Fair dates (July 29 – Aug. 9, 2026) and words Ohio State Fair must be included.
For reference purposes, or for those who choose to include this information, 2026 will be the 171st Ohio State Fair.
The artist must disclose the medium of the poster.
Size guidelines 24 inches x 36 inches/2:3 aspect ratio. Portrait orientation required.
Must be able to be reproduced and must be scalable to standard poster or postcard size.
All entries must be submitted electronically. Submissions must be high-quality digital photographs or images of your poster. .JPGs and .PDFs are acceptable.
Top 5 entries will be recognized.
Grand Champion will receive:
$1,000
Coordinated opportunities with local media
Daily access to the Ohio State Fair and tickets for family
