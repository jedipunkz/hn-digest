How to choose
Prompt-to-production vs prompt-to-prototype. Some tools host the result with domains and SSL handled (WeInc, 10Web, Durable); others output code you deploy yourself (Bolt, v0).
Pricing model matters more than the sticker price. Flat monthly plans are predictable; token/credit metering means heavy iteration — including retrying the AI's own failed generations — costs extra on most tools.
For agencies: white-label support (your brand, your pricing) is rare — WeInc and 10Web are the main options.
For developers: Bolt, v0 and Replit give you the code and the dev environment; the site builders trade that control for zero-ops hosting.
WebContainers — the browser-side Node.js tech behind Bolt-style builders
Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui — the styling stack most AI builders generate
Supabase — the backend most AI app builders pair with
Awesome No-Code — the broader no-code/low-code list
Maintained by the team at WeInc . Contributions welcome — open a PR to add a tool (criteria: generally available, generates real websites/apps from prompts, not a thin GPT wrapper).
