CtrlVibe AI コンソール キーパッド – AI ワークフローの OpenAI Codex Micro の代替品 (クラウドファンディング)
CtrlVibe AI コンソールは、AI ワークフロー用に設計されたプログラム可能なデスクトップ キーパッドです。このデバイスには、AI ツール間の切り替え、ショートカットの起動、実行モードの選択、音声入力の録音のためのハードウェア コントロールが含まれています。 9 つのプログラム可能なメカニカル キー、ロータリー エンコーダー、3 ポジション スイッチ、1.14 インチ LCD、内蔵 MEMS マイク、USB-C および Bluetooth 接続、RGB ステータス ライトが付属しています。
CtrlVibe の機能と仕様により、OpenAI Codex 用に設計された 230 ドルのメカニカル マクロパッドである Work Louder Codex Micro の代替品となります。 Codex Micro の機能の多くを再現する M5Stack Core2 用のオープンソース ファームウェア プロジェクトも利用できます。 Codex Micro は OpenAI Codex 専用に設計されていますが、CtrlVibe AI コンソールは複数の AI ツールで使用でき、ロータリー エンコーダーを使用してアプリケーションまたはモデルを切り替えるとともに、AI 実行モードを選択するための 3 ポジション ハードウェア スイッチを使用します。

このデバイスは、CtrlVibe Configurator ソフトウェアを使用して設定され、ユーザーはキー、ロータリー エンコーダー、および許可スイッチに機能を割り当てることができます。ロータリー エンコーダーを使用すると、ChatGPT、Cursor、VS Code、Gemini、ターミナル セッションなどの AI アプリケーションを切り替えたり、さまざまな AI モデルを循環したりできます。さらに、Ask、Bypass、および Auto モードを備えた 3 ポジションの許可スイッチがあり、ユーザーが実行前に確認を要求したり、確認なしでコマンドを実行したり、確認が必要なタイミングをソフトウェアに決定させたりすることができます。

CtrlVibe AI Console keypad – An OpenAI Codex Micro alternative for AI workflows (Crowdfunding)
The CtrlVibe AI Console is a programmable desktop keypad designed for AI workflows. The device includes hardware controls for switching between AI tools, launching shortcuts, selecting execution modes, and recording voice input. It comes with nine programmable mechanical keys, a rotary encoder, a three-position switch, a 1.14-inch LCD, a built-in MEMS microphone, USB-C and Bluetooth connectivity, and RGB status lighting.
The features and specifications of the CtrlVibe make it an alternative to Work Louder Codex Micro , a $230 mechanical macropad designed for OpenAI Codex. There is also an open-source firmware project available for the M5Stack Core2 that reproduces many of the Codex Micro’s features. While the Codex Micro is designed specifically for OpenAI Codex, the CtrlVibe AI Console can be used with multiple AI tools and uses a rotary encoder to switch between applications or models, along with a three-position hardware switch for selecting AI execution modes.
CtrlVibe AI Console specifications:
Display – 1.14-inch color LCD screen for status, battery, token usage, and AI companion display
Audio – Built-in near-field noise-reducing MEMS microphone array for voice-to-prompt input
Keys – 9x standard 1U mechanical keys (hot-swappable low-profile switches)
Encoder – 1x Stepless rotary knob (EC11) for tool and model switching
Connectivity – Bluetooth and 2.4GHz Wireless
USB – USB Type-C port for power and PC connection
Misc
3-position stainless steel toggle switch for permission modes
Full-color RGB workflow status lighting (Blue for idle, Purple for processing, Green for complete)
Enclosure
Top Case – 6061 aluminum alloy (CNC unibody) with a brushed anodized deep space gray finish (Optional Carbon Matrix plate)
Bottom Case – Multi-layer acrylic (smoky gray + light guide layer + black base layer) over a rigid ABS bottom shell
The device is configured through the CtrlVibe Configurator software, which lets users assign functions to the keys, rotary encoder, and permission switch. The rotary encoder can be used to switch between AI applications such as ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini, and terminal sessions, or to cycle through different AI models. Additionally, there is a three-position permission switch with Ask, Bypass, and Auto modes, allowing users to require confirmation before execution, run commands without confirmation, or let the software decide when confirmation is needed.
CtrlVibe Configurator software allows key mappings, shortcuts, operating modes, and target applications
We have written about various programmable macropads and shortcut keypads over the years, from ESP32-based macropads like the duckyPad Pro to commercial products such as CH552G-based mini keyboards , UltraBar X desktop smart-hub , and Waveshare MK10 . Unlike those devices, the CtrlVibe AI Console is designed specifically for AI-assisted workflows, supporting switching between multiple AI tools and controlling AI execution via dedicated hardware controls.
The device comes with nine programmable mechanical keys that can be configured for different shortcuts and workflow actions using the companion software. It also supports hot-swappable mechanical switches, making it easy to replace the switches to suit different preferences.
Exploded view of the CtrlVibe
Default functions assigned to the nine programmable keys include Record, Run, Stop, Apply, Retry, Agent, Model, Diff, and Prompt.
For voice input, the CtrlVibe uses a built-in MEMS microphone and speech processing software to convert spoken commands into text. According to the company, the software can remove filler words, format the transcribed text, and send it directly to the active AI application. Voice processing can be performed locally on the host PC or through an optional cloud service.
CtrlVibe’s 1.14-inch LCD connection status
The CtrlVibe AI Console is now available on Kickstarter , with Super Early Bird pricing starting at $109 for the standard model and $129 for the Carbon Matrix Edition. Standard Early Bird pricing starts at $139, with additional bundles that include accessories such as a lavalier microphone, replacement switches, and a carrying case. Shipping costs will be collected separately after the campaign, and deliveries are currently estimated for November 2026. At the time of writing, the CtrlVibe AI Workflow Console software is not publicly available, and will be made available when the campaign ends.
