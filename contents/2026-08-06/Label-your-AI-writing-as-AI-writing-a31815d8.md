Label your AI writing as AI writing | Raymond Xu's Blog home blog Label your AI writing as AI writing
If you have a blog on your website, just fucking tell us which part is AI written.
I'm actually fine with people telling the AI to do some research, make a report and putting it on the internet.
I enter these blogs or posts presuming things are human written, only to be dissapointed when I see the first em dash.
If content is labelled as AI generated at the top, I actually can enjoy it if I feel that the deep research being done does succinctly explain something.
On a blog that's titled like an opinion piece, I'm looking for human opinions, not AI opinions. AI opinion pieces are just aggregate averages of other opinions on the internet after all.
But! As I run a startup, I do want to generate reports of, for an example, my startup compared to other startups in the space. If there are 100 different startups, this is a lot of blogs! And maybe some are useful, and so I will make them public. But, I label them as AI generated so that people know that the writing and comparisons does actually reflect something I believe in. See screenshot below of how I've done this.
No shame in thinking your AI research or your AI conclusions may have value to others, just don't imply that it's your own opinion.
