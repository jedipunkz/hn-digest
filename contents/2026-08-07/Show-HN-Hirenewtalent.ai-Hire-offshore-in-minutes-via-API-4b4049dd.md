How quickly can I hire a virtual assistant?
You can get matches within about a minute after sharing your requirements. If a candidate is already pre-vetted, you can hire immediately, and some clients have hired in less than 5 minutes. If you move forward with the full process, we typically complete interviews and present vetted candidates in about 5 business days.
How much does pricing start at?
Pricing starts at $650 per month for 80 hours and $1,200 per month for 160 hours. Final pricing depends on role complexity, required skills, and seniority.
A Virtual Assistant (VA) is an offshore worker who can support functions like sales, email and calendar management, customer support, operations, data entry, research, social media management, and more. VAs typically cost much less than domestic resources while delivering comparable performance.
What is included in the monthly price?
Your monthly price includes the billable labor hours, sourcing, vetting, interviews, compliance, contracting, payroll management, and ongoing support through the platform. Additionally, you receive free replacements and best practice sharing free of charge.
Is there a long-term contract?
No. Plans are month-to-month, so you can start quickly without committing to a long-term contract.
Can I interview candidates before I decide?
Yes. You review vetted talent and can interview shortlisted candidates before making your final hiring decision.
What happens if the hire is not the right fit?
We offer free replacements and can provide replacement options quickly so your operations can continue without a long hiring gap.
