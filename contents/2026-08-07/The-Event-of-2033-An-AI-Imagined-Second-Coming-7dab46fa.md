THE EVENT OF 2033: An AI-Imagined Second Coming
What would happen if the Second Coming unfolded in the age of artificial intelligence, global media, and the cloud?
On Easter Sunday, April 17, 2033 , a radiant figure appears above Jerusalem. Screens across the world are interrupted. Markets freeze. Governments lose control of the narrative. Every eye turns toward the sky—and humanity must confront the possibility that history has reached its most extraordinary turning point.
The Event of 2033 is a work of speculative fiction presented as a dialogue between Nicolás Halaban and an artificial intelligence. Drawing on biblical imagery, apocalyptic literature, technology, geopolitics, and celestial symbolism, the conversation constructs an ambitious imagined chronology of the Second Coming.
What would the first seven days look like? How would social media, financial markets, religious institutions, and political powers react? Could resurrection be physical as well as spiritual? Would Christ rule from Jerusalem? What might become of modern Israel, global empires, national debts, the natural world, and humanity itself?
Across thirteen chapters, the book explores:
• The worldwide appearance of the Son of Man
• The first week following the Event
• Resurrection and the judgment of souls
• The final conflict between the Lamb and the Beast
• The Millennial Kingdom and the healing of the Earth
• Ezekiel’s visions, the Temple, and the throne in Jerusalem
• The judgment of nations, economies, and empires
• Gog and Magog and a complete speculative chronology
Provocative, imaginative, and often unsettling, The Event of 2033 is not a prediction, doctrine, or claim of divine revelation. It is a literary experiment: an AI-imagined vision of what might happen if the symbols of Scripture suddenly entered the technological world of the twenty-first century.
