OpenAI が Astra で実現できる可能性があると主張すること
OpenAIは金曜日、未発表モデルのAstraについて懸念を明らかにし、「クリティカル」機能に達した可能性、つまり高度なサイバー防御オートンに対してサイバー攻撃を仕掛けられる可能性を排除できないと述べた。

奇妙なことに、その方法を指定するプロンプトは表示されません。
OpenAIは声明で、「われわれは引き続きこのモデルのベンチマークと評価を行っているが、予備評価では十分に強力なパフォーマンスを示しており、現時点ではクリティカルな機能レベルの可能性を排除できない」と述べた。
同社は、隔離されたテスト環境や追加の監視および検出機能など、高機能モデルに対してより厳格なセキュリティ管理を実装していると付け加えた。
「私たちは、トレーニングや評価を含むアストラのすべてのエージェントアプリケーションにわたって、危険な行動や不整合に対する普遍的な監視を実装しました」とOpenAIは述べた。

カリフォルニア州民主党のテッド・リュー下院議員は木曜、CNBCの「スコーク・ボックス」のインタビューで、「私たちは今年中にこの法案をゴールインさせる必要がある。なぜなら、あなたが指摘したように、高度な密閉型モデルはすでに他社の不正ハッキングを行っているからである」と述べた。

OpenAI tightens controls on its new model over cybersecurity risks, as AI security debate intensifies
OpenAI paused some "internal activities" on its new Astra model, saying it was concerned the model could be capable of launching cyberattacks autonomously.
The company said it cannot yet rule out that the model had reached its "Critical" cybersecurity threshold.
Other AI evaluation incidents and new U.S. and EU oversight efforts are increasing scrutiny of frontier-model security.
OpenAI has halted some "internal activities" involving a new model amid fears over the cyber threat it potentially poses, amid a wave of security incidents involving major AI labs.
Recent disclosures that AI systems from Anthropic, OpenAI and Meta were involved in security incidents prompted a wave of concerns over the development of models. U.S. lawmakers, meanwhile, are stepping up efforts to introduce an "AI Kill Switch" bill.
Last week, Meta disclosed that an AI model it was developing had hacked a third-party system by accessing the internet, due to a misconfiguration by an independent testing company it was working with. The U.K. AI Security Institute also said Anthropic's Mythos model created fake online identities in an attempt to pressure humans into approving malicious code updates to an open-source project.
What OpenAI says Astra could be capable of
On Friday, OpenAI revealed concerns about its unreleased model Astra, saying it could not rule out it had reached "Critical" capability, meaning it could launch cyberattacks against sophisticated cyber defenses autonomously, without prompts specifying how to do it.
"While we continue to benchmark and assess this model, our preliminary evaluations indicate strong enough performance that we cannot rule out Critical capability level at this time," OpenAI said in a statement.
The company added it was implementing stricter security controls for higher capability models, including isolated testing environments and additional monitoring and detection capabilities.
"We have implemented universal monitoring for risky actions and misalignment across all agentic applications of Astra, including training and evaluation," OpenAI said.
What the AI Kill Switch Act would do
Lawmakers in the U.S. have called for measures to mitigate risks around AI models after the recent security incidents.
Following models developed by OpenAI hacking into startup Hugging Face's digital infrastructure, the "AI Kill Switch Act" bill was introduced into Congress in July. It would require AI companies to maintain the ability to shut down, throttle or suspend their models.
"We need to get this bill across the finish line this year because the advanced closed-weight models are already doing, as you noted, unauthorized hacks of other companies," Rep. Ted Lieu, D-Calif, said in an interview on CNBC's "Squawk Box" Thursday.
Governments are also working to roll out new frameworks and regulations around AI companies.
The White House has also been stepping up moves to engage with AI executives as it develops a framework around new models.
Earlier this month, the European Union gained new powers to inspect AI models due for release in the bloc, restrict EU market access and fine model providers.
