Transformer Lab Sign in Research About Primus is the world’s first fully autonomous AI researcher available to everyone.
Every innovation we rely on today comes from research: experts read the literature, propose a theory, run the experiments, and publish their findings. This is the research loop , and until now it has always run at human speed where one turn could take months.
Primus runs the same loop 30× faster. It autonomously hypothesizes, reads millions of papers, codes, runs experiments on real compute, learns and iterates, draws conclusions, delivers artifacts and writes the final paper. The more experiments Primus runs, the better it gets.
We ran Primus for 30 days. It wrote more than 30 complete papers, each on a question never answered before, across LLMs, seismology, physics, crystallography, and biology. Findings that Primus helped produce have already been cited in published research from labs like DeepMind.
