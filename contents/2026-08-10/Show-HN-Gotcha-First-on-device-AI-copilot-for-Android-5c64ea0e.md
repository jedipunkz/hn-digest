Gotcha is a fully on-device AI copilot for Android — control your phone with natural language through 100+ device tools, dual safety modes, and voice calls over any app.

Skip to main content Projects Personal Guru
On-device Android copilot · Available now
Most AI projects talk — Gotcha acts. A fully on-device AI copilot for Android that turns natural language into real device actions across 100+ tools, keeping you in total control.
Bring your own model (local or cloud)
Free Samosa AI credits to start
Simply state what you need in everyday language. Gotcha evaluates live device context and triggers the exact Android system operations required to complete your task.
Gotcha runs as one of two copilot modes on your device. Switch modes instantly from the interface at any time.
Reads screen context, inspects device state, and analyzes information. It cannot send messages, alter settings, or delete files — ensuring zero-risk inspection.
Performs direct actions across messages, calls, apps, media, and screen automation — fully bounded by explicit permissions you grant.
> visual feedback & permissions
Clear permissions & visual mode indicators
In call mode, Gotcha gives clear visual ring indicators: a Blue Ring means it is reading the screen and performing foreground operations, while an Orange Ring signals background processing. Actions are protected across 4 permission levels so you are always in control.
Tier 0–1 Everyday Permissions Battery, storage status, phone dialer, opening apps
Tier 2 Personal Access Calls, SMS, calendar events, location, camera access
Tier 3 Device Automation Screen touch automation, notification reading, device admin
Tier 4 Privileged Access Root shell actions (strictly fails closed on unrooted devices)
A floating companion accessible from anywhere on your phone. Long-press it — or just say “Hey Gotcha” — to start a push-to-talk voice call, where Gotcha sees your screen and acts on your behalf.
Example: Browsing Amazon and see a shirt you like on a model? Ask Gotcha to “find similar shirts to the one worn here.” Gotcha takes over the screen, searches the web, and presents matching products instantly.
Gotcha is available now as an APK on GitHub Releases , with in-app updates from then on. Sign in with Samosa AI for free starter credits, or bring your own local or cloud LLM.
