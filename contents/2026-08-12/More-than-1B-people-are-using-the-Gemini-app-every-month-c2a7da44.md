More than 1 billion people are using the Gemini app every month.
The Gemini app has officially surpassed 1 billion monthly users, making it the fastest-growing product in Google’s history. Here’s some data about how people are using Gemini:
Voice is transforming daily interactions: 63% percent of users now talk directly to Gemini, including more “voice only” users. And busy parents are 43% more likely to use voice for everyday tasks.
Gemini helps in the physical world: One in five Gemini Live interactions go beyond voice; people are using live camera feeds and screen sharing for real-time problem-solving. This is really popular with DIYers and students.
Students upload all the time: 38% of school requests include an attachment.
Small businesses love Gemini’s creative features: Gemini now generates 150 million+ images every day. It lets you do images, video, and audio in one place, helping small businesses create products like marketing materials.
Android users put Gemini to use: Gemini can automate actions across 40+ popular apps — great for booking rides, reserving tables, and more.
Apple users are turning to Gemini: There are 100 million+ active users on iOS, and macOS power users prompt around two times more frequently than other surfaces.
As we keep building for the next billion, our goal remains the same: to make Gemini the most personal, proactive, and powerful assistant.
