Some of the increase is likely due to uncertainty created by tariff policies last year. That said, there has been little improvement even as uncertainty has faded, suggesting that other factors like AI may be at play.”
Is biofuel putting up the price of food?
About 3.5 million barrels of biofuel are burned every day, mostly for transport in the U.S., E.U., and Brazil, which consume about 80% of all biofuel, according to Daan Struyven and his team at Goldman Sachs. As the Gulf crisis has pushed up the price of oil, government mandates for biofuel—a blend of ethanol (from corn or sugarcane) and diesel; or diesel and vegetable oil—have also risen. “In the U.S., the share of soybean oil used for biofuels has risen from 40% in 2020 to ~55% in 2026,” Struyven said in an email. With fewer of those crops showing up in the food system, food prices have gone up as a result, as the above chart suggests.
NUMBER OF THE DAY: Time billionaires
The number of years that have elapsed after 1 billion seconds. If you believe you have more than 31.7 years of life left, then you are a “time billionaire.” The term was popularized by Graham Duncan, co-founder of East Rock Capital , on the Tim Ferriss podcast in March 2019. Duncan uses it to persuade young people that their wealth is best measured in time left on this earth, and that therefore the sooner you start investing, the better (in order to take advantage of the miracle of compounding). It’s another way of expressing the concept “time in the market beats timing the market.”
