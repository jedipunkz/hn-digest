🎓 🇬🇧 The A3 Delegation System Founding Workshop — September 28-29, 2026
Your team already delegates work to AI: reports, research, customer feedback analysis, stakeholder communication, or parts of operational workflows.
But can you answer these questions without improvising?
What may AI decide, and what must remain a human decision?
What does “good enough” mean for this particular work?
Who verifies the result before somebody acts on it?
