群衆スコアはどのように機能しますか?

How does GLAD-AI-TOR rank AI tools?
Every ranking is computed live from real visitor verdicts across six arenas (voice, LLMs, image, video, coding and music): 76 tools compared, one verdict per person per tool, and nobody can pay for a spot.

How does the crowd score work?
Every visitor can cast one verdict per tool (recommend or avoid) with a short comment, once per tool. Scores are smoothed with a Bayesian prior so a tool with 3 votes can't outrank one with 300. Votes are never paid or sponsored.
