Boundaries
Capture is meant to be impossible to miss: the overlay, the dimming, the moving stroke. If you don't see all three, it isn't happening.
The screen is read only when you hold the shortcut and draw. Never on a timer, never in the background, and never of itself: ScreenForm's own windows are excluded from every capture.
OCR and context gathering never leave your Mac. Captures live in a per-session temp folder and are deleted when the session closes.
Free requests go through our proxy, so no provider key ships inside the app. Your own key lives in the macOS Keychain.
No microphone. No camera. Screen Recording is the only permission it needs. It reads no files of yours, and the only place it writes is the ~/ScreenForm folder, when you ask it for one.
