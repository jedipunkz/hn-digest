## Original Extract

Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude your everyday questions in the language you are learning, and get replies plus grammar corrections. Ready-made prompt inside.

How to Use AI as a Language Learner | Vocably How to Use AI as a Language Learner
If you're a language learner who's been thinking about using AI in your target language, you've found the right place.
My personal concern with using AI was: what if I make a mistake? How would I know?
To solve this problem, I created a universal prompt that you can add to ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. With it, the assistant doesn't just help you — it also evaluates and corrects your grammar.
Follow these 3 steps to set it up
Works with ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude.
1. Which language are you learning?
2. Select and copy this global prompt.
Select and Copy to Clipboard 3. Add the copied prompt to your AI assistant's context.
Paste the prompt into the Custom instructions section of ChatGPT Personalization .
Open Gemini personal context , tick the Your instructions for Gemini checkbox, then click + Add and paste the prompt.
Paste the prompt into the Instructions for Claude section of your Claude profile .
Not gonna lie, it will be a bit difficult at the beginning. You will have to compose yourself to write your prompts to AI in a more-or-less sane way. But the more you do it, the better and easier it becomes.
I have also created browser extensions that play out with this method extremely well.
Language acquisition through real-life usage
If you actually use your target language in daily life, these tools and techniques are for you:
Online Dictionary — a dictionary created specifically for language learners
AI Grammar Checker — the only checker that not only corrects your grammar, but also explains why
