Hey folks, I bootstrapped "More Good Reviews" to $170,000 in sales before building this. I just launched an MCP for AI reputation and review management. You can find it in Claude's connector directory: https://claude.ai/directory/more-good-reviews Connect your Google Business Listing and get reports with action items on what your customers are saying and what you need to fix. It comes complete with over 40 tools, prompts, and skills: https://github.com/moregoodreviews/mgr-skills With an account, you can do a lot more:
- Schedule review requests over email or sms to your customers
- Reply to your Google or Facebook reviews
- Add reviews to a widget on your website
- Spot positive and negative trends across many locations
- Get actionable next steps on where to improve based on customer feedback There is also an agency plan. Marketing agencies can white label the product and offer the MCP under their own domain name to their own customers. Would love to hear what you think!
