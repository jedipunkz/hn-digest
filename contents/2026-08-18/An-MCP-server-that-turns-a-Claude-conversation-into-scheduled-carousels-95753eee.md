Carousel Identifiers: embedPostId vs designId
Generation produces two distinct IDs. embedPostId identifies the generation job and is used by postnitro_check_status and postnitro_get_output. designId identifies the durable design artifact and is what scheduling expects — passing an embedPostId where a designId is expected fails with a "Design not found" error. Every tool that returns a completed carousel surfaces a top-level designId to bridge generation into scheduling.

One convention worth knowing: when a design's output is PDF and the post targets LinkedIn, the correct postType is document (with a 5–90 character postTitle) rather than carousel. postnitro_generate_and_schedule applies this automatically; the other schedule tools pass your choice through as-is.
