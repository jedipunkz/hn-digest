Is this post hypocritical? It sure is.

Do Not Talk About Large Language Models Elijah Potter
Do Not Talk About Large Language Models
Plainly, they are bor­ing.
It’s all any­one ever talks about any­more.
AI this and AI that .
It’s all the same dis­cus­sion.
Peo­ple read some­thing on­line, twist it into their own ﬂa­vor of opin­ion, and re­gur­gi­tate it to the masses or for their boss.
It’s tir­ing.
I do it too.
I talk about LLMs a LOT .
It is a part of my job to know about them and to have an opin­ion on them.
It is a part of every soft­ware en­gi­neer’s job to­day.
It’s okay to have an opin­ion, and it’s okay to share it with oth­ers.
I have no gripe with that.
But for your own per­sonal san­ity, I im­plore you to write about some­thing other than LLMs.
People, in­clud­ing my­self, have plainly be­come tired of the dis­course around them.
My re­cent com­plaint about it , and the re­sult­ing dis­course on Hacker News , is ev­i­dence of that.
Large lan­guage mod­els been dis­cussed to death.
Just be­cause every­one else is writ­ing about them, does not mean you need to write about them too.
Show your orig­i­nal­ity and write about any­thing else .
Is this post hyp­o­crit­i­cal? It sure is.
