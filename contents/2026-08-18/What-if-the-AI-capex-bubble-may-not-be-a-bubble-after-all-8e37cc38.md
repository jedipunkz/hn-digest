Sam Altman is being proved right about the economics of AI
There are two reasons to doubt whether cheaper Chinese challengers such as Kimi K3 will catch up to the power of US frontier models such as OpenAI.
The way Satya Nadella tells it, Sam Altman had a killer pitch when first discussing an OpenAI-Microsoft partnership: “knowledge is the log of compute.”
In other words, human knowledge – about anything – is an increasing function of the amount of computing power thrown at it. And knowledge increases more and more slowly as more compute is applied.
