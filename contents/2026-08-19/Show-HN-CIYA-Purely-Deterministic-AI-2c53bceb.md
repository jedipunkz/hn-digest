CIYA is an AI infrastructure layer that eliminates recurring token costs, runs on legacy hardware, and delivers full state resolution in under a second. Air-gapped, no hallucinations, audit trails built in.

Hey HN, Before we roll out our fully deterministic compiled language model (CLM), figured I'd post CIYA's v0.02 for you to try out. At this point CIYA acts as a storage and logic engine for any of your prompting needs, and is available via API/Websocket integration. If your company/project frequently repeats the same prompts, needs to template out those (or any) prompts, or wants to keep your business logic directly on site instead of giving it away to cloud providers, CIYA can do all of that for you. Feel free to pull up the guide using the command R: guide to see the current list of available tools like the ability to run applications, model your own datasets and build your own independent response tables. We've developed a clear path for CLM integration, allowing for a fully compiled language model that can resolve up to 1 million tokens (on-prem) in under 200ms without ever having to access an LLM. Feel free to checkout our landing page to get in touch: https://iiio.co , or run R: contact in app to fill out the contact form. Open to any feedback, comments or questions you may have!

How to use this beta portal?
Use CIYA till your heart's content. As it stands, CIYA is running in front of a mix of Cloud Providers. Good news though! If one, two or all of them go offline / time out, CIYA still functions properly. All the added tools and public datasets survive all LLM hangups / disconnects. Granted, you may be restricted from creating unique new content at that time, but this is why CIYA works perfectly in air-gapped, on-robot / on-prem environments. Without an LLM to run, CIYA still provides you with all her functionality, until the LLM's you've specified are available again. Wowza!
