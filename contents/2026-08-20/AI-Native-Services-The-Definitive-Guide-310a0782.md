An AI-native services (AINS) company uses AI to deliver complete services, not software, faster and better than traditional providers. The customer buys an outcome, not a tool. Emergence Capital named the category in 2024.
A SaaS company sells a tool and the customer does the work. An AINS company sells the outcome itself, with AI doing the work a person or firm used to do, and the vendor holding responsibility for the result.
Emergence Capital coined the term “AI-native services” (AINS) in 2024 to describe companies that use AI to deliver outcomes rather than sell software as a tool. Sequoia Capital has since written about the same shift in its 2026 piece “Services: The New Software.”
