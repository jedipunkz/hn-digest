Twitch はオンライン ビデオ ストリーミング プラットフォームであり、Amazon の子会社です。人々は主に、自分がカメラに映りながら、プレイしているビデオゲームをライブストリーミングするためにこれを使用します。他の人も視聴したり、お互いにチャットしたりできます。 AP通信は、AmazonがAIモデルのトレーニングにユーザーデータを使用していることを懸念している。
Twitch のライブ ストリームにはゲーマーの顔、声、名前が表示され、多くの場合、寝室などのプライベート スペースの画像が含まれます。これは個人データを構成します。顔画像の場合、これには機密の個人データも含まれます。これらのデータが Amazon の AI システムに保存されると、簡単に削除することはできません。その結果、ユーザーは自分のデータを制御できなくなります。ユーザーのチャットやテキストメッセージは、Amazon のトレーニング資料としても機能します。

AP advises Twitch users: opt out from sharing data with Amazon AI
The streaming platform Twitch announced that Amazon is using Twitch users’ personal data to train AI models. Users who do not want Amazon to use their streams, images and other personal data can now opt out from this feature. The Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (AP), the Dutch data protection authority, is urging people to do so.
Twitch is an online video streaming platform and a subsidiary of Amazon. People mainly use it to live-stream the video games they are playing, whilst being on camera themselves as well. Other people can watch and chat with each other. The AP is concerned about Amazon using user data to train AI models.
Live streams on Twitch show the gamer’s face, voice and name, and often include images of a private space, such as a bedroom. This constitutes personal data . In the case of facial images, this even involves sensitive personal data . Once these data are stored in Amazon’s AI systems, they cannot simply be removed. As a result, users lose control over their data. Users’ chats and text messages also serve as training material for Amazon.
The ‘Training for Generative AI’ setting is enabled by default. This means that Amazon automatically uses data from all Twitch users to train its AI, unless users disable the setting. To do this, go to: Settings, the Security and Privacy tab, under the ‘Training for Generative AI’ option.
Monique Verdier, Deputy Chair of the AP: “Sharing data such as your face and voice to train Amazon’s AI models is very risky. You won’t be able to delete this. AI models could, for example, disclose your data to online scammers, who would then misuse them. In a statement made by Twitch it says that this setting is enabled by default, because otherwise nobody would give their consent. That says enough. So, make sure to change it today.”
