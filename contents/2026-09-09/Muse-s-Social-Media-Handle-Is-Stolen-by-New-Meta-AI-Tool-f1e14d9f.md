Muse Give Up Their Social Media Handle to AI in Ironic Twist
Muse appeared to have lost their coveted social media handles to an unlikely new rival: Meta’s artificial intelligence tool. The switch created an amusing twist for a band famous for warning about technology taking over.
The British rockers had used @Muse across social platforms for years. Now, Meta’s new personal AI agent had claimed the name, leaving the band with noticeably less sleek handles.
Muse changes its Instagram and X handles after Meta’s AI launch
The Independent reported that Muse’s X account had changed to @Musetheband. Their Instagram handle had also become @museband. Meta’s new Muse AI app had taken the band’s former @Muse name on both platforms.
Meta launched Muse on Tuesday for adults in the United States. The personal AI agent was designed to handle tasks like scheduling, shopping, payments and emails. It could also turn “long-term goals into action plans.” The tool was built around privacy and safety, according to Meta. Users could access it through a standalone app or WhatsApp. It could also browse, fill out forms and handle certain tasks independently.
That made the handle switch especially funny for Muse fans. The Devon-formed band had spent decades exploring technology, surveillance and dystopian futures through its music. Their 2018 album Simulation Theory leaned heavily into those themes. On “Algorithm,” Matt Bellamy sang about humans becoming trapped inside technological systems.
The band’s 2015 album Drones also explored technology and social oppression. Fans quickly spotted the delicious irony surrounding the new AI takeover.
“Lmao, zuck took the “muse” handle on Instagram for his new AI thing, and now the world-famous band with nearly 3m followers that’s been around for 25 years has been given museband,” one fan wrote on X.
Another reacted, “Can’t believe an ai slop app stole the handle from a real human band.”
Some fans even wondered whether Muse had been paid for the handle. However, there was no confirmation that a financial deal had occurred. The Independent said it contacted representatives for both Muse and Meta for comment.
Muse had reportedly made the social-media changes in July. Some fans also claimed their accounts automatically followed the Muse AI account, although that could not be verified. At the time, the AI account had around 11,000 followers, compared with the band’s 2.3 million.
Fronted by Bellamy, Muse remained one of Britain’s biggest rock acts. The band had released 10 studio albums, including June’s The Wow! Signal, and had a UK and European headline tour scheduled from November. The run included London’s O2 Arena.
