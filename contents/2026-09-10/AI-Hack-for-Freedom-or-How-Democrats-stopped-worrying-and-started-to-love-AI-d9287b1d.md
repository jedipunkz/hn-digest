AI Hack for Freedom — receipts
AI Hack for Freedom is an HRF-backed hackathon series that pairs AI developers with political dissidents and opposition activists to build working tools for their movements. HRF and Finite have trained participating activists on AI agents before the hackathon, and AI Freedom Lab has said standout projects may receive follow-on funding.
Hack III is recruiting developers for Washington, DC on September 24–25. The reason this deserves scrutiny is the documented funding, personnel, and historical-program overlap with the NED/USAID/NDI democracy-promotion ecosystem, plus the unresolved question of who can reuse the resulting tools afterward.
1. HRF describes political AI capability-building
HRF says the hackathon brings together human-rights/democracy advocates and open-source developers to build tools supporting movements under repression.
Before Hack II, HRF and Finite ran an Agent Camp where activists deployed coding and personal AI agents. AI Freedom Lab said standout tools would be considered for future funding.
https://web.archive.org/web/20260828060319/https://hrf.org/latest/hrf-sponsors-second-edition-of-ai-hack-for-freedom-in-nashville-tn-may-9-10/
2. Hack III winners event is at NED headquarters
Hack III's winners ceremony is being held at the National Endowment for Democracy's Washington office:
Drinks + Food courtesy of HRF, NED and AI Freedom Lab.
https://web.archive.org/web/20260828060529/https://luma.com/9xz32f1a
3. NED funded organizations represented by hackathon captains
NED's FY2024 Form 990 reports cash grants of:
$160,488 to World Liberty Congress
$237,105 to Ideas Beyond Borders
https://web.archive.org/web/20260311101958/https://www.ned.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/National-Endowment-for-Democracy-FY-2024.pdf
World Liberty Congress supplied captains to both hacks:
Leopoldo López , co-founder — Hack I
Félix Maradiaga , president — Hack II
Archived HRF Austin announcement:
https://web.archive.org/web/20260828060601/https://hrf.org/latest/hrf-sponsors-ai-hack-for-freedom-in-austin-tx-jan-17-18/
Archived HRF Nashville announcement:
https://web.archive.org/web/20260828060319/https://hrf.org/latest/hrf-sponsors-second-edition-of-ai-hack-for-freedom-in-nashville-tn-may-9-10/
4. Faisal Al-Mutar / Ideas Beyond Borders
Faisal Al-Mutar was a Hack I captain . HRF identifies him as founder and president of Ideas Beyond Borders.
https://web.archive.org/web/20260828060601/https://hrf.org/latest/hrf-sponsors-ai-hack-for-freedom-in-austin-tx-jan-17-18/
NED's FY2024 Form 990 reports $237,105 to Ideas Beyond Borders:
https://web.archive.org/web/20260311101958/https://www.ned.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/National-Endowment-for-Democracy-FY-2024.pdf
5. Joey Siu / former NDI staff
Joey Siu was a captain at Hacks I and II.
NDI's December 2023 statement explicitly called her a "staff member" :
https://web.archive.org/web/20260828060641/https://www.ndi.org/publications/statement-arrest-warrant-issued-hong-kong-government-against-staff-member-joey-siu
Hack I identifies her as Spokesperson, Amnesty International Hong Kong Overseas :
https://web.archive.org/web/20260828060601/https://hrf.org/latest/hrf-sponsors-ai-hack-for-freedom-in-austin-tx-jan-17-18/
Hack II identifies her simply as a Hong Kong activist :
https://web.archive.org/web/20260828060319/https://hrf.org/latest/hrf-sponsors-second-edition-of-ai-hack-for-freedom-in-nashville-tn-may-9-10/
The defensible description is therefore former/documented-2023 NDI staff , not current NDI staff in 2026.
6. Srđa Popović / Otpor / CANVAS / USAID
Hack II captain Srđa Popović co-founded Otpor and later founded CANVAS.
USAID's own final evaluation of its Serbia-Montenegro Office of Transition Initiatives program says its pre-2000 activities included:
direct assistance to OTPOR and DOS
It also says OTI worked with OTPOR on election-related activity, leveraged funding from NDI and IRI, and coordinated meetings among civic groups and opposition political parties.
Wayback copy of the USAID PDF:
http://web.archive.org/web/20230520185017/https://pdf.usaid.gov/pdf_docs/pdabx440.pdf
Older Wayback capture of the same USAID PDF:
https://web.archive.org/web/20060930125807/https://pdf.usaid.gov/pdf_docs/pdabx440.pdf
Independent preservation record for USAID DEC document PD-ABX-440 :
https://decfinder.devme.ai/dec/final-report-final-evaluation-of-otis-program-in-serbia-montenegro
Congressional Research Service report independently citing and summarizing the same USAID evaluation:
https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/20090527_R40600_92252119ff05220fd70aadb14edeca632184f42c.pdf
HRF lists Popović as a Hack II captain:
https://web.archive.org/web/20260828060319/https://hrf.org/latest/hrf-sponsors-second-edition-of-ai-hack-for-freedom-in-nashville-tn-may-9-10/
7. Randi Weingarten / NDI / No Kings
Randi Weingarten is a current NDI board member:
https://web.archive.org/web/20260828060701/https://www.ndi.org/people/randi-weingarten
No Kings' March 24, 2026 release lists her, in her separate role as President of the American Federation of Teachers , among:
national leaders and organizers from the No Kings coalition
https://web.archive.org/web/20260828060728/https://www.nokings.org/news/over-3000-no-kings-events-planned-for-march-28-more-events-added-daily-ncgpw
This is a personnel overlap . It is not evidence that NDI funds, staffs, or directs No Kings.
This is political capability-building for real opposition and dissident movements with documented funding, personnel, and historical-program links into the NED/USAID/NDI democracy-promotion ecosystem.
The unresolved issue is downstream control :
Once reusable political AI tools, models, code, or workflows are built, who can subsequently receive or reuse them?
I found no evidence that these tools have been transferred to domestic extremist or violent groups. The public material reviewed so far simply does not answer what controls would prevent that.
former staff means former staff;
personnel overlap means personnel overlap;
none of those facts alone proves direction or control.
