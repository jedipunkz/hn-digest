**Jot is a privacy-focused AI writing assistant and text rewriter for Chrome.** Rewrite emails, messages, replies, comments, posts, and announcements directly where you write.
No copying text into ChatGPT. No switching tabs. No complicated prompts.
**Jot helps you:**
* Rewrite and improve your writing
* Make text shorter, clearer, or more professional
* Rewrite emails, messages, replies, and social posts
* Adjust your writing style with custom instructions
* Use different writing personalities for different websites
* Refine a rewrite without starting over
* Bring your own AI provider and API key
### Privacy first
Your text should stay yours.
Jot is designed with privacy in mind. Sensitive information can be **redacted on your device before your text is sent to an AI provider**, then restored locally after the rewrite.
Jot also does not store your text or rewritten content on Wurkflo servers.
### Write where you already write
Jot works directly in your browser. Use it with Gmail, Slack, Reddit, LinkedIn, and other websites without opening another AI chat or copying text between tabs.
### Your voice, your AI
Create custom writing styles for different websites. Use Wurkflo or bring your own AI provider, model, and API key.
**Write naturally. Jot makes it sendable.**
