Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard for connecting AI assistants to tools and data. Bloomiro MCP is a server at https://mcp.bloomiro.com/mcp that brings your live Bloomiro project into Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, and VS Code. Your AI can read the same Google and AI visibility data, scans, competitor gaps, and prioritized tasks that power Bloomiro's proprietary Optimization and Action Layer in the dashboard.
The Optimization and Action Layer is Bloomiro's proprietary system on top of visibility analytics. The Optimization Layer is where teams view what to optimize and change. The Action Layer turns recommendations into workflows, next steps, and create actions. MCP does not replace that layer. It connects your AI tools to the same project context so you can optimize and act inside Cursor, Claude, or ChatGPT.
Google Search Console (queries, pages, clicks, impressions), Site Scan and Page Scan findings, competitor scans, AI presence results across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews, community citation sources (Wikipedia, Reddit, Quora, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, reviews), prioritized SEO and GEO tasks, and sitemap search across your site or competitors.
Spreadsheets and screenshots go stale the moment you paste them. MCP returns current project state: the task list after yesterday's deploy, GSC after the weekend sync, or a competitor scan from this morning. Your assistant cites real scores and URLs from your property instead of guessing.
Founders shipping fixes in Cursor or Claude Code, SaaS marketers who research in ChatGPT but need live visibility data, agencies where each teammate connects their own MCP key to shared org projects, and teams doing GEO who want AI mention checks next to traditional SEO tasks.
MCP connects to your personal Bloomiro account via OAuth or your own API key. Organization projects, competitors, and scans follow your org settings. Private tasks and page scans stay private to you and the org owner. You choose which site to authorize during connect. Multi-site users call get_project_context to resolve a domain or site_id, then pass site_id explicitly to later calls.
Cursor, Claude (custom connector), ChatGPT (custom app with Developer mode), Claude Code, Codex, VS Code, and Perplexity connectors. OAuth is the default path. Some clients also support a local stdio server with a token from the connect page.
Most saved-data reads, including project context, tasks, GSC, crawler policy, and the compact MCP guide, are free. Fresh site, page, competitor, visibility, and AI presence analyses use credits under the same rules as the dashboard. run_analysis returns credits_used with the job result.
In your Bloomiro account, open your project's MCP tab in the dashboard. There is a setup guide for each supported client. Use https://mcp.bloomiro.com/mcp as the MCP server URL when your client asks for it.
Yes. Bloomiro MCP can work with Bloomiro's Headless CMS so assistants in ChatGPT, Claude, or Cursor can draft, revise, and publish site pages with your live visibility context. For the CMS-focused walkthrough, see the guide on connecting ChatGPT and Claude to Bloomiro CMS. MCP itself connects your SEO and AI visibility project; CMS publish is a separate capability when Headless is connected.
Yes. MCP access is included on Builder and Growth plans at no extra fee. You still need a Bloomiro subscription and project; MCP connects to your own account, not a shared demo workspace.
Examples: "What are my open SEO tasks?", "How do we compare to competitors on AI visibility?", "Which Reddit or YouTube pages do AI answers cite for us?", "Scan this URL for GEO issues", "What queries drove impressions last month in GSC?", or "Run AI presence on our buyer prompts."
Ask for a Community Sources overview first, then the page list for a platform when you need URLs. Prompt text is optional so responses stay small. It reads your latest completed AI presence run and does not start a new crawl by itself.
