Quebec's Artificial Intelligence Institute, Mila, and an AI company building in Montreal's Mile-Ex neighbourhood were vandalized Tuesday night, police say.
Windows at the research institute that studies AI deep learning and nearby software company ServiceNow were smashed. A graffiti reading “ Burn the Data Centers" was also painted on the side of one of the buildings.
The vandalism comes as All In, one of Canada's largest AI and tech summits, gets underway on Wednesday in Montreal.
Mila's founder, the prominent computer scientist and AI researcher Yoshua Bengio, is set to speak at the event on Thursday. ServiceNow's vice president of AI research Valérie Bécaert, is also a speaker.
The summit, which is sold out, is expected to bring together over 6,500 AI experts and industry stakeholders over two days at the Palais des congrès in the city's downtown.
Plan for mega data centre in Sherbrooke, Que., raises transparency, environmental concerns
A spokesperson for the Service de police de la Ville de Montréal (SPVM), Manuel Couture, said officers on patrol discovered the vandalized buildings around 11:45 p.m. on Tuesday.
He says they then arrested a 25-year-old woman near the scene and brought her in for questioning.
Couture says police suspect a group of about 10 individuals is responsible for the acts of vandalism.
The SPVM is investigating the case.
