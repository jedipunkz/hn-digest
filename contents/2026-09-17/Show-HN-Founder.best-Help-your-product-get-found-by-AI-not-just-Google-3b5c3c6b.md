Founder.best is a launch platform and community for founders. Indie makers submit their products, the community upvotes the best ones, and the top launches each week climb the leaderboard. How long does my product stay on the homepage?
Standard Launch stays live for 7 days from your launch date. Launch Pro and Pro+ stay visible for 30 days. After that, your product page and backlink remain - browse the full catalog anytime on the all-time leaderboard. How much does it cost to launch a startup on Founder.best?
Listing your product starts at $2.99 for a Standard Launch - live on the homepage for a week with community upvotes, a dofollow backlink, and newsletter inclusion. Upgrade to Launch Pro for a premium badge and priority placement. How is Founder.best different from Product Hunt?
Founder.best is built specifically for indie founders. It includes a founder leaderboard, founder stories, dofollow backlinks for SEO, embeddable badges, and a focused weekly cycle so smaller launches still stand a real chance to win. Do you give a backlink to my startup?
Paid launches (Standard, Launch Pro, and Launch Pro+) include a dofollow backlink on your product page. Free launches get a nofollow backlink. Sponsored spots also link with dofollow. Every plan includes an embeddable founder badge. More on pricing & plans .
