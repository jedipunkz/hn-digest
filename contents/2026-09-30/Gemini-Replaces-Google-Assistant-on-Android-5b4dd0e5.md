Gemini fully replaces Google Assistant on Android
34 Comments
At the start of September , the Google Assistant deprecation on mobile got underway. It now looks to be widely rolling out.
Google has been moving Assistant users to Gemini over the past few weeks. This deprecation was originally set for 2025 and later extended to 2026. In early August, Google announced the final timeline.
From various user reports today, this migration appears to be fully underway. Google Assistant is gone on all devices/accounts we checked in the US and Europe.
Liked by 30 people
Gemini can't even figure out a simple navigation request in Android Auto. "Navigate to John Smith's house" (for which John Smith is in my contacts with an address) is too much for Gemini to fathom. However with Assistant, it worked flawlessly.
Notably, the Gemini app has removed the “Switch to Google Assistant” setting. This shortcut no longer appears at the bottom of the account menu with Google app 17.60 stable and 17.62 beta.
As such, you can no longer “use or switch back to Google Assistant on your phone, tablet, or paired devices.” The latter grouping includes Wear OS, headphones, and Android Auto.
There are no changes to Assistant just yet on “Google Nest and Google Home smart speakers, displays, or other Google Assistant-enabled speakers and displays.”
Gemini is now the “voice assistant experience on Android” when you long-press the power button or swipe from the corners.
