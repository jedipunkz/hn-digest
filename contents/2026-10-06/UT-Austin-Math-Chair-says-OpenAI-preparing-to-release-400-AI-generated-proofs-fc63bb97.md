Mathematics does not grow simply by accumulating correct statements. Results have to be understood, connected, explained, challenged, reused. Until that happens, they risk remaining lettera morta.

So my question is genuinely: what is the intended mathematical value of releasing hundreds of proofs at once? What happens if there simply aren't enough people willing or able to read them?
