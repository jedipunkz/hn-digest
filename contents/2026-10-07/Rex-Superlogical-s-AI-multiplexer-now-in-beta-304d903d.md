Public testing is beginning — Superlogical
Public testing is beginning
Rex is ready for public testing. We're beginning to invite members of our mailing list today.
Rex is a drop-in replacement for whichever terminal you use today and you will feel an instant upgrade
in user experience, quality, and capabilities such as persistent sessions,
performant and responsive remote connections, program activity status,
and more. Plus, it's simply beautiful.
Initially, we're limiting testing to our macOS application.
The macOS application self-hosts its own Rex server and it is able to connect
to other Rex servers. If you have multiple macOS devices, you can run Rex
on all of them and connect to any through the macOS application.
Rex is not limited to macOS. We already have Rex servers running on Linux
and Windows, and we have Rex clients for iOS, Linux, and Windows at varying
stages of readiness. We hope to expand the scope of public testing
quickly, but they aren't part of testing yet.
We ran a small private testing program with about 50 people and we've been
successfully using Rex every day for real work for months. We are confident
in the quality of what we've built.
That being said, public testing always turns up environments and use
cases we didn't anticipate, so while the product has been incredibly stable
for us and our small private testing group, we're sure to find edges we'll
fix quickly through the test program.
Rex is and will always be free.
You do not need to create an account. You can fully self-host your
Rex servers on any platform (initial testing is limited to macOS, as noted
above). We do not see or share your data.
We're not ready to share our commercialization plan today, but we'll
talk more about it prior to the stable public release. Rest assured, the
Rex clients and servers themselves are not going to be directly monetized,
won't require an account, won't share data, and can be entirely self-hosted.
Invites will go out in increasingly large waves as we prove out product
stability, but our intention is to work through the entire list in less than
a month.
The test program requires signing up to the mailing list. When we're ready for
a stable public release, you will be able to download Rex directly without
sharing any contact information.
Sign up to secure your place in the waitlist
We'll let you know when our beta for the terminal multiplexer is available, and any
OSS releases along the way.
