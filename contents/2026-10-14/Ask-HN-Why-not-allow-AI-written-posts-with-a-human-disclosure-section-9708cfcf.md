HN text: Some research papers already have an "AI disclosure" section where the human authors indicate what they contributed and what the AI contributed to the paper. ## Original Extract

Some research papers already have an "AI disclosure" section where the human authors indicate what they contributed and what the AI contributed to the paper. AI-written posts on social media could similarly have a "human disclosure" section where the AI indicates what the human user contributed and what the AI contributed to the post. Note that the content of the "human disclosure" section would be determined and written automatically by the AI based on its conversation with the human user. What do you think of this idea?
