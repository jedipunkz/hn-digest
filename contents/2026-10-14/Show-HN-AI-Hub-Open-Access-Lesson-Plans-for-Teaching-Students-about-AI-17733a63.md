Open-access resources from six nonprofits, helping educators in Allegheny and Westmoreland counties teach students about AI.

The AI Hub provides 91 curated lesson plans teaching middle and high school students about AI - how it works, what the risks and benefits of AI are, and how it is impacting society. These activities come from six different nonprofit organizations that are building educational resources for classrooms. Our nonprofit worked with 16 different teachers to comb through more than 200 different resources, with the teachers vetting these resources to find the best resources for their classrooms. The end result is 91 different lesson plans and activities that cover a wide range of different topics. Schools across Pittsburgh will be using these resources over the course of this year to help teach their students about AI, and they will be scaled up nationally over the coming years. If you're a human that has thoughts or feedback, please drop a comment! What's top of mind: "What else should we teach students?"
