The amounts of money being thrown around here are just staggering. A year and a half ago, the AI boom was still significantly smaller than the railroad boom of the late 1800s, as a percent of GDP. Now, there’s an active argument as to which one is bigger.

Source: Bloomberg For all of the hyperscalers except Microsoft, free cash flow either went negative in recent months or is expected to go negative late this year. This has been a giant boon for semiconductor companies — Nvidia, TSMC, and the rest:
Source: Bloomberg All this debt creates a financial danger that didn’t exist before now. When they paid in cash, hyperscalers were safe — if AI revenue disappointed, their stock prices would go down, but their businesses would just keep going and they’d eventually recover their profitability. But the more hyperscalers borrow, the more revenue they’ll need just to stay solvent; if the revenue doesn’t arrive, they might have to default on their debt.
