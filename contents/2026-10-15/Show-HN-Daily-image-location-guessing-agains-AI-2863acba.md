## Original Extract

Guess where five daily photographs were taken and compare your score with AI models. One game a day, five photos, and a running score of humans against models.

Every day, I let all frontier AI models play five rounds of an image location guess game. Try it out how you perform against the AI. I recently released my browser image location game, a hobby project where you’re get random images from sources like Wikimedia Commons and have to guess the image’s location. There are an incredible number of images with rich metadata on Wikimedia Commons, most of them depict trivial but interesting places or events. My goal was to make this online catalog playable and to make it easier to discover new places and Wikipedia articles. I realize I’m certainly not the first person to have this idea, but I’ve tried to build a cohesive overall concept around it with multiplayer, content filter, own image collections and more. I thought it would be interesting to see how well AI models do at guessing images, and I have to say, they're very good. To make it a little more interesting, every user can also take a guess at each image. I hope you find it interesting and enjoy guessing. I know it's not really a fair comparison. In the end it's more entertainment then a real competition. Please feel free to ask any questions and I look forward to any suggestions/feedback.
