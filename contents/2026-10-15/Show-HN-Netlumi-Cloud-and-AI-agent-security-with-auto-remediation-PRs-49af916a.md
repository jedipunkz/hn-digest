Netlumi is code-to-cloud and AI security for AWS, with Azure and GCP in Early Access. We scan your cloud accounts and your code for misconfigurations, attack paths and shadow AI, then open pull requests with the fixes.

We find threats and attack paths in your cloud and open a pull request with the fix in your IaC code. All you need to do is review and merge. Each PR says what was checked, and we validate our fixes before we send them. Nothing is ever applied for you.
