## Original Extract

Pareto frontier lines are used a lot for comparing tradeoffs between two different attributes in AI models.
I want to be able to see the Pareto frontier planes for 3 attributes (which probably look closer to a bumpy dome, similar to how pareto lines are actually more like bumpy curves).
And although you wouldn't be able to visualize a pareto cube/tesseract for 4+ attributes, it might still be useful in some way.
