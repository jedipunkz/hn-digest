Your CTO asks: Is it safe to let in?
Q: Can Doozy see our passwords and API keys?
A: No. Every request runs in its own sandbox, and your Slack, GitHub and connector keys stay outside it: they're added to each call on its way out. A secret goes in only when Doozy asks for it by name and says why.
Q: Can it change things without asking?
A: It works like a teammate, so it doesn't stop for approval. You decide what it can reach: tools that delete or overwrite start switched off, and you can switch off any other. On GitHub it opens pull requests but can't merge them or push to your main branch.
Q: Who sees what's asked in private channels and DMs?
A: Doozy answers where it's mentioned, in that thread. It learns only from public channels, or when someone asks it to remember something. Activity in the web app shows who asked in a private place, never what.
Whoever pays asks: What will it cost us?
Q: What happens when we use up the month?
A: Owners hear at 80%. At 100%, Doozy finishes what it's working on, starts nothing new and says so in the thread. Nothing's deleted. Wait for the reset, add a usage pack, change plan, or turn on extra usage with a cap you set.
A: Each request uses a share of the month's allowance, and its answer says how much. Requests that fail on our side are free, and so is telling it to stop. On your own AI key a request uses far less, since your provider bills the AI.
A: Doozy's AI, included in every plan and chosen by us for this kind of work. If your company prefers a particular model, any paid plan can bring its own key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or DeepSeek, and Doozy runs on that instead.
The team asks: Will it fit how we work?
Q: Do we need an engineer to set it up?
A: No. Add it to Slack, connect your tools from the browser, and mention it in a thread. That's the whole setup.
A: Slack and GitHub, ready-made connectors for tools like Linear, Notion, HubSpot, Stripe and Sentry, and any tool with an MCP server. It can read the web and use a browser too. It lives in Slack; Teams and WhatsApp aren't supported yet.
Q: What happens after the free trial?
A: Doozy pauses until an owner picks a plan. Nothing's deleted: its Memory, your Skills and every connection are there when you're back.
