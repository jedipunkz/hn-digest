Even when you approve access to an account, your AI agents don’t see your passwords. Everything in your vault remains protected.
Will an AI agent see my passwords?
No. 1Password for Agents lets AI agents sign in without receiving or seeing the credential. When an agent reaches a sign-in form, it uses a unique digital key to verify its identity to 1Password. The person sees which credential it needs and why, and then chooses whether to approve the request. That approval happens on the person’s own device, confirming that the permission came from them. 1Password then fills it directly into the sign-in form instead of sending it to the agent. Once signed in, the agent continues the task while the password stays protected in 1Password.
Can an AI agent access my whole vault?
No. Connecting an agent does not give it access to everything in your vault. It requests a login for a task, and you decide whether to approve that request. The agent can’t see the credentials, and your other saved passwords in your vault remain protected by end-to-end encryption.
Do I control which login an agent uses?
Yes. You review the access request and can approve or deny it. If more than one saved login matches, you can choose which one 1Password uses. How often you are asked to approve access may vary by integration.
How does Agentic Autofill work?
Agentic Autofill helps a supported agent sign in through a browser without handling the password itself. When the agent requests a login and you approve it, 1Password fills the password or one-time code into the sign-in page so the agent can continue its task.
What can an agent do after it signs in?
An agent may be able to see information or take actions within the signed-in account. Its behavior there depends on the agent and the website. Review the task and the account before approving access.
Which AI agents work with 1Password?
1Password for Agents works with supported personal and coding agent integrations, rather than every AI tool. Check the integration cards on this page for available and coming-soon connections. Features may vary by agent.
Who can use 1Password for Agents?
The new personal agent integrations are being introduced for 1Password Individual and Families accounts. Availability and setup requirements can vary by integration, region, device, and sign-in method. Check each integration’s details before getting started.
Do I have to use an AI agent with 1Password?
No. These integrations are optional. If you do not connect an agent, you can continue using 1Password as you do today.
