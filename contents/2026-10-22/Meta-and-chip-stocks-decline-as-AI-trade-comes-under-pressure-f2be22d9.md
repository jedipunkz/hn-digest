Good morning. Stocks declined on Thursday as semiconductor names fell, with the AI trade unwinding following quarterly results from Meta ( META ) and Tesla ( TSLA ).
Shares of the EV maker recovered early trading losses while Meta sank 6%.

Shares of the wafer equipment maker Lam Research ( LRCX ) fell as much as 10% after management pointed to "supply chain constraints" heading into 2027.
