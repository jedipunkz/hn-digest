Cutcrow is a desktop video editor for Windows and Linux where
your AI agent does the editing. You tell Claude Code or Codex
what you want in plain words, and it cuts, captions and
animates your clips on a real timeline that you can see,
adjust by hand and undo.
Do I need Claude Code or Codex?
Yes. Cutcrow doesn't include an AI model, so you're never
charged twice for AI. Connect Claude Code with a Claude
Pro or Max plan, or Codex with a ChatGPT Plus or Pro
plan. Pay-as-you-go Anthropic or OpenAI API keys work too.
Cutcrow walks you through connecting either one.
Does editing use up my Claude or ChatGPT usage?
Yes. Your agent does the editing, so it counts toward your
plan's limits (or your API bill) like any other Claude Code or
Codex session. To make your plan go further, pick a lighter
model in the model picker above the chat.
Yes. Everything your agent does lands on a real timeline with
video, picture, text and audio tracks, where you can trim,
split, reorder, retime and drag anything yourself. Each
request becomes a change card, so you can undo it in one
click.
Does Cutcrow work on Windows, Mac and Linux?
Cutcrow runs on Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) and
11, 64-bit, and on Linux as an AppImage that runs on
Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Linux Mint, Pop!_OS, Fedora, Arch
and most other distributions, with everything it needs inside.
There's no Mac version right now.
Are my videos uploaded anywhere?
Cutcrow never uploads your media. It has no accounts and no
tracking, and speech is transcribed on your computer. Your AI
agent sends what it works with (your messages, transcripts and
any frames it asks to see) to Anthropic or OpenAI under your
own account, as in any Claude Code or Codex session. The
privacy policy has the details.
Yes, and it doesn't run out. Download Cutcrow
and use it without a key: the editor and the agent work, but
exports are up to 720p with a small “Made with Cutcrow” mark,
you can have up to 3 projects, and your agent uses three of the
skills that come with Cutcrow. It's for personal, non-commercial
use. The comparison has the details.
When you want more, buy Cutcrow once for $34 and paste your
key in Settings → License. The limits go away right away, with
nothing to reinstall, and your projects carry over. All sales
are final except where the law requires a refund, so try the
free version first.
How many computers can I use it on?
Up to 3. Paste your licence key once on each computer. It
needs the internet for that one step, then works offline, and
Cutcrow remembers it after updates or a reinstall. To move to
a new computer, email us and we'll free up a spot.
Can I use Cutcrow for client or commercial work?
Yes, with a licence: it covers personal and commercial work.
The free version is for personal, non-commercial use.
Everything you make and export is yours either way.
The Windows installer and the Linux AppImage update themselves
from inside Cutcrow. When a new version has downloaded, restart
Cutcrow to install it, or carry on and it installs when you
close Cutcrow.
I lost my licence key. Where do I find it?
It's in your Gumroad receipt email and in your
