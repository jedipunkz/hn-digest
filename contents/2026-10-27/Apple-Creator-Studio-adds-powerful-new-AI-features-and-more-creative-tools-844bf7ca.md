Pixelmator Pro introduces Generate Fill, which lets creators transform selected areas of an image using natural language. Keynote, Pages, and Numbers introduce new AI features in beta, including assistants 2 that understand document context and new Model Context Protocol (MCP) support for workflow automation. 3 They also add stock video in the Content Hub and new premium themes and templates.
Apple Creator Studio updates will be available on the App Store this November as a free update for existing subscribers. 4

Editors can also tap into the Content Hub directly within Final Cut Pro and access a library of Apple-curated stock videos, photos, and backgrounds to fill gaps, add polish, or spark new ideas without leaving their workflow.

Editors can open any image from their timeline directly in Pixelmator Pro, take advantage of advanced editing tools, and save changes back to the project automatically — no reimporting required. With advanced image generation, users can create stunning images from text descriptions right inside their project, apply quick edits, and adjust visual styles. And Final Cut Pro introduces powerful workflow improvements, including more keyframe easing options for finer animation control, the ability to disconnect clips from the primary storyline to place them anywhere on the timeline, and 23 new transcription languages, making it easier than ever to caption and search footage.
Motion also adds a built-in set of powerful MotionVFX plug-ins for cinematic color grading, 3D camera tracking, and one-click selection for stunning visual effects. Users can now generate and edit images from text descriptions, send images round-trip to Pixelmator Pro, fine-tune keyframe easing, apply an all-new Displacement filter, and explore an interactive demo project. Additionally, Compressor introduces the ability to encode Cinematic mode video recorded on iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo.
