Article text:
Writing Contests That Allow AI
See also Award-Winning AI Literature .
FULLY GENERATED PROSE IS ELIGIBLE
These competitions allow all or almost all of the prose to be generated by AI.
🇯🇵 Short story Sogen SF Short Story Award · 18th edition, 2026 call · rules via Kobonabi
Tokyo Sogensha explicitly accepts stories whose prose is entirely or almost entirely generated by AI. Writers must explain where and how they used AI and be able to provide the complete process, including prompts and outputs.
Purse: ¥300,000. Pocket watch; magazine, ebook, and audio publication. The magazine fee is included in the cash prize.
Status: Open; closes January 12, 2027.
🇨🇦 Short story Inkshift Writing Contest · 2026
An contest for stories of 1,000 to 10,000 words. The rules explicitly allow human writing, AI assistance, AI generation, or any combination of the three.
Purse: US$1,000 to one winner. Feedback for finalists; optional publication on Inkshift’s blog.
Status: Open; closes October 31, 2026.
🇺🇸 Novel / novella The Prompty Awards · 2026 call; first awards in 2027
Requires at least half of the final prose to be AI-generated. English-language novels and novellas of 10,000 words or more are eligible, including works made entirely with AI. Entrants disclose the tools, approximate percentage of generated text, and their own creative contribution.
Purse: $1,000 grand prize; $500 Gold and $100 Silver in each of six categories. Optional publishing deal with Apateon Press for the grand-prize winner; software subscriptions. Prize details .
Status: Open; closes December 31, 2026.
🇺🇸 Short story UCSB AI/Human Creativity Contest · 2024
UC Santa Barbara invited stories written by humans, AI, or a combination of both. Judges were not told how each entry was made.
Purse: $700 for the short-story winner. Featured on the university’s Mind and Machine Intelligence website.
Status: Historical; closed April 10, 2024.
🌐 Short story Hyperstition Unslop Fiction Prize · 2026
The contest required a 500–10,000-word story generated entirely by AI, with no human-written prose or editing after generation. Entrants supplied their complete prompt setup. Official results .
Purse: $10,000 grand prize; six finalists received $500 each.
Status: Completed.
🇩🇪 Short story Mensch und Maschine · 2023
The Max Planck Institute for Biological Cybernetics and Schwäbisches Tagblatt invited short stories of about 1,500 words based on three AI-related scenarios. The announcement explicitly allowed entrants to have an AI text generator write their story. Max Planck Institute announcement and results .
Purse: €1,200 first, €500 second, €300 third, plus newspaper publication.
Status: Closed October 30, 2023; results published.
🇪🇸 Microfiction Microrrelatos e Inteligencia Artificial · 2023 and 2026
The Public University of Navarra and the Tracasa Chair invited Spanish microfiction of up to 150 words. The 2023 rules explicitly allowed stories created with full, partial or no assistance from generative AI. That edition’s theme was AI and Navarra. The 2026 edition used different wording, allowing stories partly or fully inspired by generative AI, with Navarra and music as its theme. 2026 official regulations .
Purse: €400 first, €250 second, €150 audience prize.
Status: The 2023 edition closed October 10; the fourth edition closed October 12, 2026.
🇨🇳 Short story GenWorld / ZhenFund AI Story Competition · 2023, plain-text entries
The second edition accepted plain-text stories under 5,000 Chinese characters, following an inaugural AI microfiction contest in August. The entire story had to be AI-generated, without human editing. Entrants could use any model or tool, but had to provide screenshots or video documenting the process.
Purse: Noncash. First prize included a game console or VR headset and US$25,000 in AWS discount vouchers; two second prizes offered US$15,000 in AWS discounts each, and three third prizes US$4,500 each. Winners also received game-adaptation and publication benefits. The cloud discounts are not cash.
Status: Historical 2023 competition. The second edition was announced in November.
HUMAN-AI COLLABORATION IS WELCOME
These contests invite AI into the writing process. Some require human revision or rule out entirely generated submissions.
🇫🇷 Fiction / nonfiction Prix Pascal de Littérature IA · 2026
A French-language prize built around human-AI collaboration. Entrants submit a manuscript of about 300,000 characters, a short seed for the work, and a two-page technical account of how it was made. The jury considers literary quality and the collaboration itself. The rules set no percentage limit on generated text.
Purse: No cash amount published. Publication and media visibility.
Status: Open; closes November 30, 2026.
🇯🇵 Short story Nikkei Hoshi Shinichi Award · 2021–2026; current rules for the 14th edition
The 2021 rules already accepted AI entries with a human representative. By 2024 , generated prose had to be revised by a human, with prompts and before-and-after versions kept for review. The current rules retain those requirements. The general division accepts Japanese stories of up to 10,000 characters.
Purse: ¥1,000,000 general-division Grand Prix. Trophy; other awards include goods and book vouchers. The junior Grand Prix is ¥100,000 in book vouchers, not cash.
Status: Closed October 6, 2026; results expected February 2027.
🇯🇵 Fiction AI Novelist Literary Award · 2022 and 2025
A Japanese prize specifically for work made with the AI Novelist writing tool. The first edition in 2022 accepted fiction and shorter forms including poetry and scripts, with an account of the process. The second edition, in 2025, received 223 entries.
Purse: ¥500,000 top prize in both editions; other awards of ¥100,000. Twelve months of AI Novelist Platinum membership for these winners. Prize details .
Status: Historical; second-edition winners announced in 2025.
🌐 Novel opening / screenplay Voltage Verse · 2025
A competition from the WritingWithAI community for novel openings and screenplays. Entries had to use AI, but could not be entirely AI-generated. Writers supplied a paragraph explaining their process. Novel entries could be up to 5,000 words.
Purse: $550 first prize in each of the novel and screenwriting categories. Writing-software and community memberships; packages differ by category.
Status: Historical; closed August 21, 2025.
🇯🇵 Serial novel Piccoma Novels Prize: SMARTOON Original Novel Contest · 4th prize, special edition, 2026
This Japanese novel competition distinguishes AI-written text, AI support that leaves no generated text in the manuscript, and writing without AI. AI rewriting of human prose counts as generated text, even after further human edits. Generated writing is eligible if the author contributes creatively and keeps process records. The submitted novel must contain at least 20 episodes and 60,000 characters.
Purse: No fixed cash prize published. Paid serialization through manuscript fees and revenue sharing; SMARTOON adaptation is subject to contract and production requirements.
Status: Open; closes November 4, 2026.
🌐 Essay Essay Architecture Prize · 2025 submissions; 2026 results
An English-language essay competition that explicitly allowed AI for brainstorming, drafting, and editing. Entrants completed a survey about their process, and AI use was not penalized. The organizers described the goal as augmenting the writer rather than automating the work. Essays could be 400 to 10,000 words and respond broadly to life in 2025.
Purse: US$10,000 grand prize. Anthology publication and a royalty share for the winner and selected finalists.
Status: Historical; submissions closed November 23, 2025.
🇲🇾 Screenplay Future Stories: AI Script Writing Competition · 2022
SeaShorts, Wu Ben Film Magazine, and the University of Nottingham Malaysia invited Southeast Asian writers to write scripts with AI. Writers could use any AI writing application. The published selections credited tools including NovelAI, InferKit, and HyperWrite.
Purse: RM1,000 for the best script. Selected scripts showcased through the organizers.
Status: Historical; 2022 competition.
🇨🇳 Short fiction / nonfiction October magazine’s “县@智” homecoming-writing contest · 2025
The Chinese literary magazine invited young writers to work with DeepSeek or another AI on stories and nonfiction about returning home. Writers had to explain the AI’s role and their own revisions. The call said the amount of AI involvement would not affect scoring, while discouraging the use of a single prompt to produce the entire piece.
Purse: CNY10,000 top prize; two CNY3,000 special awards; CNY1,000 weekly awards. Online publication, with possible selection for the magazine.
Status: Historical; closed March 31, 2025.
🇨🇦 Fiction / essay AI Pen Awards / AIFWAA · Launched 2025; 2026 cycles
A Montreal-based award program devoted to writing made through human-AI collaboration. Submissions must describe the tools, the extent of AI involvement, and the creative process. The organization runs fiction awards and an Essay Lab. Its public materials do not give a clear percentage boundary between assisted and fully generated work.
Purse: No cash amount found in the reviewed public guidelines. Award recognition and inclusion in the organization’s showcases. Prize details .
Status: Next fiction deadline December 31, 2026; Essay Lab has rolling submissions.
🇮🇳 Short story / poetry Spin Your Science, India Science Festival · 2026 competition for ISF 2027
The festival permits AI to enhance or augment stories and poems, provided writers disclose it. Fully generated entries are disqualified. The rules do not define a permitted percentage or spell out every acceptable drafting use. There are categories for writers under 15 and those 15 or older, and winners need an Indian bank account to receive payment.
Purse: INR30,000 first, INR20,000 second, and INR10,000 third in each of two age groups. Selected writing included in the festival anthology. Prize details .
Status: Closed June 30, 2026; winners scheduled December 2026.
🌐 Novel The Inkys: AI-Assisted · 2026
A dedicated category for English-language stories of at least 20,000 words first published on Inkitt in 2026. AI must have been used somewhere in the writing process. The rules do not specify a maximum AI-generated share or separately establish that wholly generated books qualify.
Purse: $500, $250 and $100; first place also receives a trophy. Additional benefits include badges and 200,000/100,000/50,000 audiobook credits.
Status: Closed October 18, 2026; winners scheduled by December 18.
🇭🇰 School writing Human-AI Creative Writing Contest · 2022 and 2022–23
The original school contest required an AI text generator and allowed students to combine generated text with their own words. The 2022–23 inter-school edition required students to design an AI writing tool on Hugging Face Spaces and submit a story of no more than 500 words containing both their own and generated words, with human-written words highlighted. 2022–23 rules and prizes ; Organizer’s edition archive .
Purse: In 2022–23, HK$1,000 first and HK$500 second in book vouchers, both overall and within participating schools. The 2022 rules page does not state a purse.
Status: Historical editions completed.
🌐 Short story AIStoryHub Short Story Contest · August 2026
Entrants had to use AIStoryHub somewhere in drafting a story of at least 5,000 words. The contest encouraged its AI writing tools but did not publish a maximum generated-text percentage. Writers remained responsible for their submissions. Official rules .
Purse: $300, $125 and $75 in Anthropic API credit, not cash.
Status: Closed August 31, 2026.
🌐 Fiction / poetry / essay / script AI Creativity Awards: Writing & Narrative · 2026
The writing category accepts poetry, fiction, scripts and essays. AI must materially contribute; entrants disclose tools, generated components and their human creative role. The policy permits prompt-led work
