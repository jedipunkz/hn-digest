It doesn't edit on its own or replace your editor. You decide what changes.
Just the Reel's video file (MP4, MOV or WEBM, up to 500 MB). You get the action plan from the video alone. If you also send the retention screenshot from Instagram Insights, publishub reads your real curve and adds a prediction you can check.
Any app that exports MP4, MOV or WEBM: CapCut, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, your phone's built-in editor and so on. publishub doesn't plug into these apps. It works with the file you export (up to 500 MB), and you apply the notes back in your editor.
