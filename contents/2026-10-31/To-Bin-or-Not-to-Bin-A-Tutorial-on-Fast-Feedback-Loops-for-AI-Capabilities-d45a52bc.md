To Bin or Not to Bin: a Tutorial on Fast Feedback Loops for AI Capabilities
Your AI agent works. Can you change it fast without breaking it?
To bin or not to bin is a hands-on tutorial where you take one small, real problem (which bins go out tonight?) from a first prompt to an agent with automatic checks, on a schedule, and finally to plain code with no model at all, using the same checks to prove it still works at every step.
It runs on your machine against a made-up council, with Claude Code, and either Apache Airflow or cron .
