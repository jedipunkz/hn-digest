Trying to understand LLMs can feel overwhelming. Unfamiliar terms appear everywhere, and looking them up one by one does not always reveal how they fit together. Then the mathematics becomes a hurdle, followed by the code.
Finding an accessible starting point can be difficult, too. Much of the latest information appears in technical documentation and research-oriented articles that assume you already know the terminology. Even when the words are familiar, the ideas behind them may not be.
This track is for anyone who has felt that way. We will explore LLMs without assuming specialist knowledge, advanced mathematics, or coding experience. The goal is to help anyone who uses AI in everyday life understand its core ideas and explain them in their own words . You should be able to follow the terms you encounter in AI news and explain to someone else how ChatGPT processes a question and generates a response.
This track takes inspiration from a collection of AI papers and resources reportedly recommended by Ilya Sutskever to John Carmack. The list brings together influential readings on the foundations of modern AI. Rather than working through the papers themselves, we will connect their key ideas into a learning path for readers without a technical background.
