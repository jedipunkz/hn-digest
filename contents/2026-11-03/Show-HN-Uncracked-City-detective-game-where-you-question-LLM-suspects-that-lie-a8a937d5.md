A detective game set in real cities. Search the places, question the suspects, catch the lie, and write the report nobody has managed to write yet.
Salem, the morning after Halloween. Meg Ashcombe, the last of her family, lies at the foot of her grand staircase, one slipper on the thirteenth step, the front door bolted from inside. Half the town says the Grey Captain finally took her.
Four suspects. One of them lies.
Every case happens in a real neighbourhood. Find the clues, open what is locked.
Ask your own questions, typed or spoken, in any of the game's six languages. Each suspect answers in their own voice. Some of them lie.
Hold a clue against a statement. When the lie cracks, write your report: three tries, not one more.
A world tour to learn. Then the real cases.
Paris, Rio de Janeiro, Marrakech, Tokyo, Sydney: five training levels, each harder than the last. Then come the real cases, harder still. Each one shows how many investigators have solved it. For most of them: nobody. Not yet.
The first time you open a case, you fly there across the map. Solve it, and its city is stamped in your passport.
A question, a bug, a suspect's line that goes wrong: write to us. In the game, “Flag” under a line sends it to us directly.
The game is played without an account. To erase everything: Settings → “Start over” → “Erase everything”. Without the phone that created your player, write to us.

## Original Extract

A detective game set in real cities. Search the places, question the suspects, catch the lie, and write the report nobody has managed to write yet.

To give you some context on the project. I'm a solo founder and I use AI heavily in my work. The idea came from wanting players to discover cities. Each city has its own history, so every case is set in a real city with real public places. The crime and the people are on other hand invented. The part that I care most is the playable aspect of the game, a model plays the detective and must solve the case before it goes live. As of today: it's free on Android, the iOS version is still waiting to be reviewed by Apple. It's available in six popular languages, but adding more languages is just a matter of time and demand.My long term vision if it takes off: weekly cases, private parties like a team-building escape game, an MCP so a game master can build their own scenario from ChatGPT or Claude, and maybe tourism offices since players travel the world.
