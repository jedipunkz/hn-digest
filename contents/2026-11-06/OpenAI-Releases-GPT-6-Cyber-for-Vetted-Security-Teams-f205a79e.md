OpenAI Releases GPT-6 Cyber for Vetted Security Teams
November 6, 2026 Technology AI
OpenAI has released GPT-6 Cyber, a cybersecurity model for approved security teams that can help find and test software flaws and develop fixes, with access limited to users who have been checked.
OpenAI says the model is available to select customers in its Daybreak Red program, which supports advanced, approved security work. OpenAI plans to expand access to other approved customers. OpenAI says the model found 81 previously unknown, severe vulnerabilities in Firefox.
It helped develop possible fixes. Mozilla has released fixes for many of them. For Linux, OpenAI reports that the model developed ways to take advantage of 948 flaws in the Linux kernel, the core software of the operating system.
OpenAI also reports that it disclosed 304 critical issues to Linux maintainers, along with suggested fixes. The company says the model followed the limits researchers had approved in 99.93% of tests when it was not given an instruction to deploy it. OpenAI says the model followed those limits in every test when it was given that instruction.
OpenAI gave the model a “Critical” cybersecurity capability rating under its safety framework. The company cited the model’s ability to find and take advantage of flaws. Access controls include identity checks and security requirements for organizations.
The limited rollout reflects the risk that powerful tools for researching software flaws could be misused.
