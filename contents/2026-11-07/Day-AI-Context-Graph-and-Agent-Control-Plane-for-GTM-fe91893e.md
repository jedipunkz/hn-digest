Customer Memory is an LLM-optimized ontology built from automatically permissioned ingestion of your team’s email, calendar, meetings, and Slack. One central context graph that you control. Vast improvements in speed, cost, accuracy, and governance.
Day agents have names, job descriptions, and titles. They live in an org chart, they hear feedback, and they get better at their jobs. A monolithic agent running frozen skills out of a repo doesn’t improve — it isn’t alive. A roster of living agents is.
And when something looks wrong at 2 a.m., you’re not the engineer on call. The audit trail says exactly what ran, and nothing went out without your approval.
Each agent has a name, a job, and skills for that job. Work is done proactively when something happens in the company, on a schedule, or whenever you ask. Every agent reports to someone on your team.
