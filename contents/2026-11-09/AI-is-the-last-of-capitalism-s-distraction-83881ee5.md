AI is the last of capitalism’s distraction
Posted October 20, 2026 & filed under AI , ecology , politics , wwweb .
A friend sends me an article that explains how an AI can predict a disease you’ll have in twenty years. Across the world, free-market peddling governments are diligently at work to dismantle public health services. At the same time a massive amount of fossil fuel burning data centers are being built, in a AI hyperscale frenzy, destroying ecosystems and communities’ well-being in the process.
It’s not AI that will destroy mankind, it’s capitalism’s insatiable greed. It’s the billionaire class that always wants more. And that obtain more through evil, undemocratic and illegal ways. Over and over again.
You don’t really need AI. Sure, it can perform fancy tasks, some of them useful. But is giving up on public healthcare, meaningful jobs, a peaceful world, ‒ or even a world at all ‒ a price worth paying for a more convenient way to write and summarize your emails?
While the world is scrambling to wrap their heads around apocalyptic announcements that AI has a 10% probability of destroying mankind, massive data centers that will consume huge quantities of water and energy are being built across the world, while large areas of carbon-sequestring ecosystems are being destroyed in the process. Thus getting us closer to (environmental) cataclysm. An actual one, backed by science , not vibes .
The US bombed an elementary school in Iran killing 120 children , and later lied about its responsibility. The “kill chain” that led to the attack included input from the Maven artificial intelligence system operated by Palantir. “[…] much of that work was condensed into several minutes via Maven.” There is a person validating the choice, but these people are known to suffer from automation bias (where humans are prone to just accepting recommendations of AI algorithms without corroborating that evidence), and will likely just rubber-stamp the AI’s decision . Whenever you use AI in your daily life, you are potentially training these kill chains .
We are immersed in hyper-capitalism, and entities playing the hyper-capitalism game always win. AI is the single, most hyped-up technology in history, by far. Massive amounts of capital have been poured into this hyper-growth, in a collective frenzy ($1.9T USD as of writing; ending hunger worldwide would cost $93B USD ). This is the biggest reason it’s everywhere, even in your dishwasher. From all the big players building tech in this space (Google Gemini, Open AI’s Chat GPT, Anthropic’s Claude, Amazon’s AWS, etc.) only NVIDIA is making money. Everyone else is losing, a lot . Money is being invested in circular ways : “Nvidia invests $100 billion in OpenAI, which then OpenAI turns back and gives it back to Nvidia.” In an effort to desperately expand revenues, these companies are racing to get government contracts, and, more specifically, with the parts of government responsible for violence and war . See previous point.
The majority of people running these AI companies are tech-billionaires that collaborate with the current totalitarian US Government. They have already spent $400 million $600 million USD in influencing US elections in 2026 . They are complicit in the deportation of US citizens based on the color of their skin, toppling democratically elected governments across the world, funding dirty wars and occupations in the middle east, killing humanitarian programs across the world. The list is massive, but this is not a list inside a list.
Your most valuable skill in adult life is being able to think rigorously for yourself. It’s a hard one to earn. Guess what will happen if you recurrently delegate reasoning to a machine? And what happens when that machine bumps up the price for your thought? In the UK, workers are already spending £1B a year out of their own money on AI tools , risking entering a dependency that will cost them even more down the line.
When you pick a technology to work with, your focus should be not only on getting the job done, but also whether said technology is future proof, or “am I getting smarter in this field by using this technology?”. AI is a black box, you don’t learn anything from it, except how to use it. It’s the opposite of a liberating or emancipating technology, it’s obscure by design. (H/T to Marta G. Franco for this idea.)
AI hallucinates, and pretends it’s alright. Ie, it delivers false results with the resolution of a university teacher. When was the last time you got savagely gaslit by an AI chatbot? Hey, at least there is no risk of starting WWIII .
Investment in data centers is praised by authorities and Big Tech companies as an opportunity for job creation. It’s a distraction. AI is here to take jobs away — even meaningful ones — and the ones added are but a small percentage of that.
These power-hungry models have been originally trained using all the data on the internet , regardless of what the creators of said data had to say about these appropriations. To me, this sounds a lot like extractivism and neocolonialism , where you harvest people’s creativity and energy instead of rare minerals or livelihoods. At time of writing, crawlers (most of them AI bots) account for more than 64% of all internet traffic , with the massive impact on the climate that goes with this. I can attest to the intensity and brutality of these AI crawlers, as the Fumaça website I am responsible for has (more than once) nearly been knocked down by an avalanche of requests , not unlike a DDoS attack . “Shoot websites down first, litigate (maybe) later” is the name of the game.
With the race to build vast amounts of data centers and the demand for hardware (chips) needed for these massive server farms to operate, the world is seeing a decrease in memory and CPUs available for other computing needs . Society goes digital, you need to be online to live, and then the cost of a simple laptop skyrockets. This is not the democratization of computing I was promised.
There are also workers forced to use AI because management met sales person or speaker sponsored by heavily-funded AI company that convinced them that AI is the only future ahead. So this is also an hierarchy problem, in which inapt management makes a call and everyone needs to obey, even if these people have strong, logical reasons to not want to use AI. This person chose to leave instead of playing this game .
I’ve been a web developer for almost twenty years now, and I opted for not using AI in my work. It’s not weird! There’s dozens of us ! I encourage you to rethink your relationship with this technology, and question yourself whether it really is something you cannot live without. Because the only inevitability in life are the laws of physics , the rest is politics (or genetics).
AI technology is not consensual among the common citizen . There are currently 3000 data centers in the US alone, and, because of generative AI’s lustful resource consumption 1500 are planned to be built. Some of these (including the ones that will power Grok, Elon Musk’s deep-fake machine) will be powered by illegal gas-burning turbines. These turbines will contribute to… You’ve guessed it, climate change! But let’s focus on that other delusional, convenient science-fiction apocalyptic message of the singularity, shall we?
So, people are getting pissed in places and communities where these data centers are being built. Their electricity bills are higher, and the quality of their water is lower. They are suddenly living next to a major source of light and noise pollution . Erin Brockovich put together a crowd-sourced map of ongoing data center expansion . In Portugal (where I’m based) people are getting together to collectively fight the take over of beautiful coastal areas for data centers, big electrical plants to power said data centers, as well as massive monocultures (avocados, oranges, etc) and the privatization of public beaches (the organization is called TAMLA). Causas de Raiz have an interactive map with locations for environmentally dangerous projects. MiningWatch Portugal is a cross-disciplinary group monitoring mining and extractivist activities. Follow and join these movements, meet real people that are fighting back, on the field. Here you can keep track of future protests, and those that are evergreen.
While searching for information and knowledge online click and visit and read websites . Remember it’s a slow and gratifying and surprising process. Like the best things in life.
I’ll wrap up with the opening screen of one of my all time favourite video games, as I think it very eloquently summarizes the AI problems for us.
Hinterland, a Canadian-based studio, creators of the The Long Dark , take a stance against the use of generative AI:
“There is no ethical application of AI, as all commercial models are built on the industrial-scale theft and exploitation of workers, as we as the destruction of our environment, to the sole benefit of the billionaire-class.”
This post was written by a human being, without recourse to any AI tools, but using the highly implicated work of countless human reporters and thinkers.
