UKA-135: A Proposed Rooted-Tree Ontology of 135 Research Domains with Epistemic Constraint
This deposit contains a pseudonymised export of a single, unplanned conversation between a user and DeepSeek's chat model, conducted over six days (19–25 October 2026). It began as a request to analyse a personal essay on forgiveness written about twenty years earlier and grew, without a prior plan, into an extended exchange covering psychological meta-analysis, a cumulative list of 143 analytical labels the model called "domains," family-history research, and a comparison of two short texts written in 2012 and 2026.
The conversation tree holds 1,788 message nodes. The final branch contains 742 user turns and 746 model turns, about 559,000 words, 52 model-initiated web searches, and 15 image uploads; a further 296 nodes sit on edited or regenerated branches and are preserved in the JSON. User turns are short (median 8 words); the model produced roughly 97 percent of the text.
This archive documents a sustained, self-directed intellectual investigation in which I used artificial intelligence as an external analytical environment. Through recursive questioning, historical comparisons, interdisciplinary integration, and repeated critical evaluation of the AI model’s conclusions, the interaction evolved from personal reflection into an extensive exploration of human cognition, knowledge organization, and artificial intelligence reasoning.
The resulting conversation demonstrates a process of human-directed inquiry and AI-generated conceptual synthesis, highlighting both the potential of extended human–AI collaboration and the limitations of AI-generated analysis, particularly regarding factual accuracy, scientific validation, and evidentiary reliability.
