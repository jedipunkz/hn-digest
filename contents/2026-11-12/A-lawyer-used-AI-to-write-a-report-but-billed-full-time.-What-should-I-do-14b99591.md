I feel like the deck is stacked against me (the lawyer also holds several prestigious positions), so I don't think I'll appeal. The risk is too high. However, Pangram since burst onto the scene, and essentially confirmed my suspicions (it flagged 1/3rd of the report as AI-generated, including the hallucinated reference). I know that the false-positive rate is higher for legal text, but I'm nonetheless more convinced than ever that I am correct in my assessment. What would you do in this situation? I'm annoyed that he got away with it, and that I don't have the time or the finances required to prove it.
