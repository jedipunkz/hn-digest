How long does the battery last?
The battery lasts up to 9 hours on a single charge. This will vary by use, configuration, settings and other factors † .
How do I charge the glasses, and how long does a full charge take?
Charge the glasses by placing it in its case. If the case battery is low, connect a charger to the case.
When you charge your device for the first time, remove the black paper from inside the case first.
You can charge your glasses to 60% in about 30 minutes. †
Can I get prescription lenses put into these frames?
You can add prescription lenses directly on your intelligent eyewear manufacturer product page. Enter your prescription details to see which lens options are available. Please visit Warby Parker or Gentle Monster for more details.
Do I need to keep my phone with me at all times for the glasses to work?
Your glasses require a Bluetooth connection to your phone to enable functions like asking Gemini about your surroundings or streaming media, while some device functions like volume control, checking battery life, or taking a photo don't require a Bluetooth connection.
Do these glasses work with both iPhone and Android?
Our glasses are compatible with Google Pixel 7 series and later running Android 17 September 2026 or later, iPhone 13 and later (min 6GB RAM) running iOS 26.6 or later, and Samsung Galaxy S or Z series with UI 9.0 or above.
Are the glasses water-resistant or durable enough for daily wear?
Intelligent Eyewear is designed for daily life and outdoor use. The glasses support IPX4 water resistance, helping protect against splashes, light rain and sweat encountered during daily activities. While Intelligent Eyewear is not intended for extreme environments or high-impact sports, its design and durability are optimized for regular, on-the-go use as part of everyday life.
