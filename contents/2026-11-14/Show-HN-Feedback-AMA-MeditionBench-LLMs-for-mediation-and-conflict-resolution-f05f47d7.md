## Original Extract

I am the founder of Human Assisted Intelligence and I believe AI can be used to reduce polarization and improve the way people communicate and reach agreement. At https://mediationbench.com/ , there are many frontier LLMs tested, but it is difficult to keep up. I've been trying to get more free openrouter or huggingface credits.
Azure for OpenAI and Anthropic have both provided token credits. (Thank You!) The research paper is there as well. There have been efforts to use LLMs to lie and play diplomacy, which are excellent compliments to this work. In v4, the test will get harder as Claude nearly saturated the test. Astra was surprisingly low, perhaps because of alignment or safety efforts. I'd love to get feedback and hear from all of you. I've been working on related ideas for 20 years, so it's definitely a passion project with too much time in isolation.
