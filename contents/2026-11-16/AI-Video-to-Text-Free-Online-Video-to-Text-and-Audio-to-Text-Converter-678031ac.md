AI Video to Text is a free online tool that turns video and audio recordings into clean, timestamped text. Simply select a file, and the built-in speech recognition model produces an editable transcript you can copy or download as TXT, SRT or VTT.
Unlike most transcription websites, AI Video to Text never uploads your files. A compact Whisper model runs entirely inside your browser, so your recordings stay private, there is no account requirement, and there are no per-minute fees.
MP4, MOV, WEBM, MKV and other video files have their audio track extracted and transcribed automatically.
MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC and more are converted into timestamped transcripts in one click.
Every transcript is already split into timed segments that export directly as SRT or VTT caption files.
How to convert video to text or audio to text
Add your file Drag any video or audio recording onto the converter, or click to browse your device.
Select the spoken language English is selected by default. Choose another language or leave it on Auto-detect.
Click “Transcribe with AI” On the first run the speech model (~39 MB) is downloaded and cached. After that, the file is processed in short segments and text appears progressively.
Review and edit Open the Transcript tab to correct names, numbers, or punctuation before exporting.
Copy or download Copy the text to the clipboard or download it as a clean TXT, SRT or VTT file.
On-device transcription vs typical upload-based tools
Most online transcription services upload your recordings to their servers. AI Video to Text takes the opposite approach — everything stays on your device. Here’s what that means in practice.
The honest trade-off: a lightweight browser model is not as powerful as large cloud models on very noisy or heavily accented audio. For clear speech it is fast, private and free.

On-device processing — your files never leave your device
When you click Transcribe, the browser decodes the audio and a WebAssembly version of the Whisper model converts speech to text on your own CPU or GPU. The only network activity is the one-time download of the model and helper libraries, which are then cached locally.
This makes AI Video to Text a practical choice for client calls, internal meetings, legal interviews, unreleased media, or any recording you prefer not to send to a third-party server.
Important: because everything runs locally, closing or reloading the tab will stop the transcription. Keep the tab open until the progress bar reaches 100%.
Getting the most accurate results
Transcript quality depends mainly on the source recording. These practices make the biggest difference:
Reduce background noise. Music, traffic, and room echo are the most common sources of errors.
Prefer one clear speaker. Overlapping voices are difficult for any speech recognition model.
Set the correct language. Manually selecting the spoken language is more reliable than Auto-detect on short or noisy clips.
Check names and numbers. Proper nouns, brands and figures are the places that most often need a quick edit.
Use a desktop for long files. More available memory makes processing of recordings longer than one hour more stable.
Select the spoken language in the converter. English currently delivers the most consistent results; other languages work well depending on audio quality and accent.
