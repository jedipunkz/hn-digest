## Translation

Title: Gemini is finally changing their privacy policy
HN text: Just received this: We’re updating your privacy settings in Gemini Apps to give you even more control over your data and help make Gemini safer. We’ll start rolling out these changes in the coming weeks. What’s changing: The Keep Activity setting currently controls how long your chats are saved and whether they are used to train generative AI models. Over the coming weeks, we will replace Keep Activity with two new settings. Using the first setting,
you will be able to control how long your history is saved. Using the second setting,you will be able to separately control whether your chat content, including text and attachments, is used to train generative AI models. With this change, you will be able to keep your history while independently controlling generative AI model training.
If you turn off your History setting, future chats will be saved for around 2 monthsand used to respond to you, process your feedback, and protect Google, its users, and the public, with help from human reviewers. How this affects your account: If your Keep Activity setting is off when this change is made, your new settings will both be off. If your Keep Activity setting is on when this change is made, your new settings will both be on. What’s not changing: You will still be able to change how long history is saved, choose an auto-delete period, and review or delete history manually at any time.
As before, Google will not use your audio or Gemini Live recordings to improve
Google services by default. You can change this at any time in settings.

## Original Extract

Just received this: We’re updating your privacy settings in Gemini Apps to give you even more control over your data and help make Gemini safer. We’ll start rolling out these changes in the coming weeks. What’s changing: The Keep Activity setting currently controls how long your chats are saved and whether they are used to train generative AI models. Over the coming weeks, we will replace Keep Activity with two new settings. Using the first setting,
you will be able to control how long your history is saved. Using the second setting,you will be able to separately control whether your chat content, including text and attachments, is used to train generative AI models. With this change, you will be able to keep your history while independently controlling generative AI model training.
If you turn off your History setting, future chats will be saved for around 2 monthsand used to respond to you, process your feedback, and protect Google, its users, and the public, with help from human reviewers. How this affects your account: If your Keep Activity setting is off when this change is made, your new settings will both be off. If your Keep Activity setting is on when this change is made, your new settings will both be on. What’s not changing: You will still be able to change how long history is saved, choose an auto-delete period, and review or delete history manually at any time.
As before, Google will not use your audio or Gemini Live recordings to improve
Google services by default. You can change this at any time in settings.
