MAI Licensed Weights: Frontier performance you control
Nov 17, 2026
· Ignite
· Models
More of what makes a company distinctive will live inside the AI models it runs. Businesses increasingly want to control those models: who can access them, how they’re adapted, what they cost to operate, and when they’re upgraded.
That’s why we’re introducing MAI Licensed Weights, giving organizations the ability to deploy and customize Microsoft AI models in their own Azure environments.
MAI Licensed Weights brings Microsoft’s frontier AI models into your own Azure environment, where you can adapt them to your business and safely integrate your proprietary knowledge and expertise. Your data and adaptations remain yours, with Microsoft only using them to train its models with your express agreement.
As these models become embedded in your most important products and workflows, you retain control over how they’re deployed, who can access them, and when they’re upgraded. That means you can innovate at your own pace, without compromising the continuity your business depends on.
Frontier performance, built for your business
The best model for a business isn’t necessarily the largest or most powerful general-purpose model. It’s the one that delivers the best performance on the tasks that matter, at a cost that makes deploying AI at scale viable.
By adapting MAI models to their proprietary knowledge and workflows, customers are achieving frontier-level or better performance on specialized tasks, at a fraction of the cost.
AT&T used MAI models to analyze 5G networks. For standard tasks, MAI outperformed GPT-6.1 Sol at just 1/50th of the cost. 1 For more complex tasks, it achieved frontier-level performance at a significantly lower cost.
Other customers have demonstrated similar results:
Woven by Toyota used MAI models to fix software code, producing ready-to-merge fixes in 44% of cases where its existing frontier models required human intervention, at less than one-tenth of the cost. 2
Alaska Airlines improved the accuracy of aircraft maintenance report classification from 81% to 91%, outperforming leading frontier models at a fraction of the cost. 3
These results show what becomes possible when companies combine Microsoft’s frontier AI research with their own expertise: better performance on the tasks that matter, at dramatically lower costs. As more of that expertise becomes embedded in AI models, the ability to control and evolve them becomes increasingly valuable.
MAI Licensed Weights is coming soon through our Frontier Company engagements. To learn more, contact your Microsoft account team.
About these results
Results reflect customer-specific evaluations of models adapted for particular tasks. Performance and cost advantages vary by workload, model configuration, and evaluation methodology.
AT&T: Customized MAI Code 2 Flash and Code 2 evaluated on 5G network operations analytics, including comparisons with GPT-6.1 Sol.
Woven by Toyota: MAI Code 2 Flash fine-tuned on historical MISRA coding-standard fixes, compared with frontier models used in production.
Alaska Airlines: MAI Code 2 Flash evaluated on aircraft maintenance report classification, with accuracy improving from 81.2% to 91.1% following tuning. Qualified employees retain responsibility for final decisions.
