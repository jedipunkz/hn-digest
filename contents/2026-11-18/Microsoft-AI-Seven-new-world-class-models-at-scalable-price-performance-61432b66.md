Seven new world-class models at scalable price-performance
Nov 17, 2026
· Announcements
· Ignite
At MAI, our mission is to build you the very best models in the world and keep every developer and every enterprise at the frontier of capability.
But as token spend skyrockets, we know that you also want models at a price point that lets you run agents 24-7 doing real engineering work.
That’s why today we’re releasing a family of seven new world class models at a truly exceptional price-performance:
MAI Cyber 2 Flash beats GPT-6 Astra’s quality at less than 2% of the cost per task on the CyberGym-E2E benchmark.
MAI Code 2 reaches 98% of the coding quality of GPT-6.1 Sol at about 40% of the price. And against Opus 5.5, it reaches 96% performance for just 5% of the price.
MAI Code 2 Flash matches the performance of GPT-6 Luna at 65% of the price, and Haiku 5.5 at 20% of the price.
MAI Image 2.7 is #1 on Artificial Analysis image editing, outperforming GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst (max) at just one-fifth of the price.
MAI Realtime 1 outperforms GPT-Realtime 2.1 and starts at just $0.01 per audio minute.
Dragon Copilot Transcribe is built on MAI Transcribe 2, the world’s fastest and most accurate multilingual transcription model, and already powers 10 million patient encounters a month with multilingual error rates halved.
Decision 1.1 is the latest decision model from Microsoft, 50x faster and 79x cheaper than GPT-6.1 Sol.
Maia 200 , Microsoft’s second-generation AI accelerator, is now in production running our largest models, achieving 1.7x more tokens per watt.
Frontier cyber defense built to run 24-7
Security, alignment and containment are the most important priorities for agent development in 2027 and beyond. That’s our top priority. We need to run monitoring and control agents defending and patching live production systems 24-7. That’s why MAI Cyber 2 Flash was designed to deliver frontier intelligence at a fraction of the cost.
Built with Microsoft’s decades of proprietary data and world class security expertise, our models help defenders stay safe in this new era where attackers have cheap and abundant offensive tools.
MAI Cyber 2 Flash builds on earlier MAI Cyber models that defenders already rely on. MAI Cyber accounts for almost 40% of scans in MDASH, and 90% of scans that started with MAI continued to use it. Our latest model is now priced 86% lower on output tokens than MAI Cyber 1.1 Flash, giving existing MDASH customers a stronger model at a lower price.
Incredible coding performance at a fraction of the cost
MAI Code 2 is built for the best price-performance for everyday enterprise coding tasks. Averaged over four widely used benchmarks that target the everyday enterprise coding tasks we see in GitHub Copilot (DeepSWE 1.1, Terminal Bench 2.1, SWE-Atlas and FrontierCode v1.1), MAI Code 2 gives you 98% of the performance of GPT-6.1 Sol at 40% of the price, and 96% of Claude Opus 5.5’s performance at just 5% of the price.
We’ve built these models to prioritize the coding work our customers do every day. They’re explicitly not yet tuned for harder problems, like fundamental science research and very long horizon agentic work.
MAI Code 2 also been tuned to generate great dashboards and visualizations. In a blind, side-by-side evaluation of frontend work, developers preferred MAI Code 2 over GPT-6 Sol 64% of the time. Try MAI Code 2 in GitHub Copilot today. Read more here .
MAI Code 2 Flash is designed for high-volume, everyday tasks like code explanations, targeted edits, routine fixes, and rapid iterations, while bringing quality at a much lower cost. It performs comparably to GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 at 65% and 20% of the price, respectively, across three coding benchmarks measuring day-to-day, high-volume routine coding tasks (DeepSWE 1.1, Terminal Bench 2.1 and SWE-Atlas). It’s available in GitHub Copilot and in a limited-access preview in Microsoft Foundry .
The best image and transcription models in the world
MAI Image 2.7 is the best image editing model in the world, taking the #1 spot on Artificial Analysis, outperforming GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst (max) at just one-fifth of the price. At text-to-image generation, our model is ranked #4, making us the second-best lab. And MAI Image 2.7 Flash brings world class image generation for high-volume scenarios at ~$10 per 1,000 images. Both are available in Microsoft Foundry here .
MAI Realtime 1 is our first live speech-to-speech model, designed for natural, low-latency conversations. In our evaluations, it outperformed GPT-Realtime 2.1 across external and Dynamics 365 benchmarks. It’s priced for enterprise-scale use, starting at a budget-saving price of $0.01 per audio minute. You can try it out in MAI Labs and register your interest here .
Dragon Copilot Transcribe , built on MAI Transcribe 2, is now live in Dragon Copilot, powering 100% of doctor–patient conversation transcriptions. It covers 10 million encounters a month, halves error rates in multilingual workflows, and is in the hands of a third of US physicians.
The leading Decision model in the market
Decision 1.1 provides model routing integrated right into your applications so that the bigger, pricier models are only used when you really need them. On the AA Decision Index Microsoft is ahead of Jev, Google and OpenAI, while being faster and cheaper. Used by 4,000 customers, it’s 50x faster and 79x cheaper than GPT-6.1 Sol.
Customize models for your business
We’ve heard again and again from leaders that no one wants to pour their IQ into someone else’s model.
And if you build on open weights, can you really trust where they came from, or how they’ll behave when you use one as the foundation for your most valuable IP and data?
We heard you. We started with Custom Models. AT&T developed an MAI custom model for 5G network operation analytics and it outperformed GPT-6.1 Sol at 50x cheaper cost. On complex scenarios, they found that Code 2 delivered frontier-level performance at a significantly lower price than alternatives.
Today, we’re taking a big step forward with MAI Licensed Weights : models under your control.
Take a frontier MAI model, customize it using your proprietary data if you want to. Then run it securely in your own Azure environment.
You control who can access it. Your data and adaptations stay yours. And we’ll work with you through Frontier Company to deliver the custom model and embed it in your own products and agents.
So that’s MAI, a family of world class models with exceptional price performance: the world’s best image editing model; the world’s best transcription model; frontier cyber defense at 2% of the cost; and world class everyday coding models at a price you can scale.
And crucially all of it with Your IQ. Your agents. Your weights. All under your control.
This is Frontier Intelligence you can trust, from the company you can trust.

MAI Code 2 and Code 2 Flash: Everyday coding at scalable prices
MAI Image 2.7: Frontier quality at a fraction of the cost
MAI Realtime 1: Leading voice AI at $0.01 per audio minute
