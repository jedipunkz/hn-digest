Free audio tools, processed on your device
SoundBench is a free set of audio tools that works inside your web browser. Every file is decoded, edited and encoded on your own device with the Web Audio API, the LAME MP3 encoder and FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly. Your audio is never sent to a server.
Because nothing is uploaded, there are no file size limits beyond your device's memory, no waiting in a queue, no account and no watermark. The tools work with MP3, WAV, FLAC, ALAC, M4A/AAC, OGG Vorbis, Opus and AIFF.
