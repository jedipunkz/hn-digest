サイバーセキュリティに関する重要なアップデート
2026 年 11 月 17 日、正体不明の人物が、特定の顧客の個人情報を含む内部の Virgin Plus システムに不正アクセスしました。影響を受ける個人情報には、名前、郵送先住所、電話番号、電子メール アドレス、口座番号、製品情報、最後の支払いの日付と金額などの口座詳細が含まれる可能性があります。クレジット カード番号や銀行口座情報などの機密性の高い個人情報は影響を受けていないことを確認しました。
このインシデントを発見すると、Virgin Plus は直ちに脅威アクターを排除し、緊急プロトコルを開始しました。私たちは引き続きすべてのシステムを注意深く監視します。私たちは義務を負います

Important Cyber Security Update
On November 17, 2026, an unknown party gained unauthorized access to an internal Virgin Plus system that included certain customer personal information. The personal information impacted may have included name, mailing address, phone number(s), email address and account details such as account number, product information and the date and amount of your last payment. We have confirmed that no highly sensitive personal information such as credit card number or bank account information was impacted.
Upon discovering the incident, Virgin Plus immediately removed the threat actor and initiated emergency protocols. We continue to monitor all systems closely. We take our obligation to safeguard your information very seriously and we have engaged the RCMP’s cybercrime unit to investigate.
We will update this page if new information becomes available.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):
Why was I only informed now?
The incident occurred on November 17, 2026. We started informing customers as soon as we confirmed that some customer data may have been impacted.
What kind of data was compromised?
Our investigation confirmed that the personal information impacted may have included contact name, mailing address, phone number(s), email address and account details such as account number, product information and the date and amount of your last payment. We have confirmed that no highly sensitive personal information such as credit card number or bank account information was impacted.
Have government authorities been notified?
We are pursuing our investigation and have engaged the RCMP’s cybercrime unit. We will advise other regulatory authorities in accordance with applicable requirements.
