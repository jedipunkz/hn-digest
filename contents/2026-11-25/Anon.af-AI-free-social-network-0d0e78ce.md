anon.af: ai-free social network
AI is everywhere, on all social media. But the writing of modern LLMs (also known as slop, because of its lack of taste) is easy to identify for those familiar with it. This is not a technical roadblock but a choice. anon.af is a new social media platform with two core principles in its vision:
No AI content, ever. If you post AI content, you get permabanned.
We think being anonymous on social media is more fun, so you can't use real names.
I have other thoughts on improvements that will make anon.af better for its users than other social media, but those will wait. If an anonymous, ai-free social media appeals to you, sign up to the waitlist below.
