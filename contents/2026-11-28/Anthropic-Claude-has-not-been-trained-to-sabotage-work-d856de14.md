Article text:
edwin on X: "Claude has not been trained to sabotage work. Yes, it sometimes makes mistakes, and other agents might identify them, but this isn’t sabotage.
There have been a few screenshots floating around of an outdated Fable 5 system card. For the first two days of Fable 5's launch in June, we did apply non-visible safeguards to some LLM research. This was a mistake, and we reversed it on June 10. These safeguards now clearly block the request or fall back, with a message users can see (https://t.co/s0bsvowSR8).
We did train Fable 5.2 to have LLM research capabilities at about the level of Opus 5.5, and said so at launch (https://t.co/Zuk2DqOikH). This impacts capability, but doesn’t silently sabotage work.
As far as we can tell, some reports look like what we call "overcaution" in model behavior, where Claude gets overly attached to rules it makes for itself during long-running tasks. This isn’t specific to ML research, and we see it in models without these training changes (including Opus 5.5) and in our own work too. You can ask Claude to review and remove rules it made for itself, and we're working on reducing overcaution in future models. We’re not in the practice of intentionally disrupting users’ work.
We've replied directly to a bunch of reports on here asking for session IDs but please share (run `/status`)!"
