Updated 11.27.2026 8:30 PM EST
This page contains updates on a security incident affecting certain user accounts and College Board products, and the steps we have taken to protect our systems and users. We will update this page as we learn more and take further action.
College Board has identified unauthorized activity affecting certain user accounts and College Board products. We are actively taking steps to protect our users and our systems.
Our preliminary assessment is that this activity involved sophisticated AI tools that have recently been leveraged to gain access to the systems of other major institutions worldwide. While our investigation is ongoing, we have determined that the activity involved access to the systems students and professionals use to create new College Board accounts and to submit CSS Profile applications for financial aid.
This access allowed the actors to access basic account information for a subset of users and to initiate password resets in some cases. Upon detecting the activity, we took immediate action to protect our users and augmented existing security measures. To date, we have no evidence of improper access to our repository of test scores and content.
Users with existing College Board accounts can still access them securely with reduced functionality. However, new accounts cannot be created, and CSS Profile is temporarily unavailable. We will let users know when these services can be safely restored.
We are communicating with the College Board community broadly to provide any necessary steps users should take in response.
