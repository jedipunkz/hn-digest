## Translation

Title: Donald Knuth suspends error bounties due to an influx of AI-generated reports
Article title: Knuth: Moratorium

Article text:
For more than fifty years I've been welcoming reports from readers
of errors in my books, so that I can correct them before new copies
are printed.
Alas, the rise of LLMs has increased the productivity of my readers
so greatly that error reports are now being submitted much faster
than I can read them!
This content is very important to me, and I wish to take personal
responsibility for getting everything right. Therefore I insist
on verifying everything carefully.
That takes time. But the amount of available time is now less
than zero.
Random sampling indicates that the vast majority of errors that
have been reported in recent weeks are repeats of issues already
submitted by others.
Therefore, I shall not be able to look at any error reports
submitted after 25 November, 2026, until I've posted an
updated list of the known errors in whatever book is involved.
Please consult the list below to see if error reports are
currently being accepted.
I apologize for having made errors in the first place, and for
having limited ability to cope with more than one thing at a time.
I shall do my best to get through the existing reports as
soon as humanly possible.
Thanks in advance for your patience and understanding.
Books for which errata are now welcome
(At the moment I'm swamped, but hoping to add items to this list soon.]
